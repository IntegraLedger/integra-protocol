**LCP profile `card/mastercard-vi`.** 1. The merchant's `checkout_jwt` payload has the member `"legalContext":{"type":"sha256","value":H,"legalContextUrl":L}`. 2. The wallet or agent fetches L, hashes, compares; on a mismatch it does not sign. 3. On a match the user's L2 (Immediate) or the agent's L3b (Autonomous) checkout mandate carries that `checkout_jwt` with `checkout_hash` = BASE64URL(SHA-256(ASCII(checkout_jwt))), and the payment mandate's `transaction_id` equals it. 4. No constraint type carries H: Verifiable Intent rejects open mandates with unknown constraint types.
