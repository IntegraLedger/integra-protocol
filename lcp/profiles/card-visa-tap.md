**LCP profile `card/visa-tap`.** 1. Before payment the merchant shows `{"legalContext":{"type":"sha256","value":H,"legalContextUrl":L}}`, where H is the SHA-256 of the ATR's exact bytes (`0x`, lowercase hex) and L an `https` URL serving them. 2. The agent fetches L, hashes what it receives and compares with H as 32 decoded bytes; on a mismatch it does not pay. 3. On a match its payment request carries one `lcp-hash` field whose value is H, and every `agent-payer-auth` signature in the request lists `"lcp-hash"`, with no parameters, among its covered components. 4. The merchant verifies an `agent-payer-auth` signature in its TAP recognition, and refuses a request in which any `agent-payer-auth` signature does not list the field, or whose H it did not issue for this checkout or has seen claimed. 5. The card authorization does not carry H; the merchant's processor reference may carry `lcp:sha256:H` as its own record.
