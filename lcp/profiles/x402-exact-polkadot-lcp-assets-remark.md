**LCP profile `x402/exact/polkadot/lcp-assets-remark`: the ATR hash in a payer-signed remark, batched with the transfer.**

*Scope.* x402 v2, `scheme` `"exact"`, `network` `polkadot:68d56f15f85d3136970ec16946040bc1` (Polkadot Asset Hub) or `polkadot:67f9723393ef76214df0118c34bbbd3d` (Westend Asset Hub), `extra.assetTransferMethod` `"lcp-assets-remark"` (required). It defines this transfer method until x402 or MPP defines Polkadot. The default flow is `authorization`, and `upfront` is allowed.
1. The option gives `amount` in the asset's atomic units, `asset` as the `pallet-assets` asset id in decimal, `payTo` in SS58, and `maxTimeoutSeconds` of at most 3,600. `PaymentRequired` carries `extensions.legalContext.info` = `{"type":"sha256","value":H,"legalContextUrl":L}`, where H is the SHA-256 of the ATR's exact bytes as `0x` lowercase hex and L is an `https` URL that serves them.
2. The client fetches L, computes SHA-256 over the bytes received, and compares the result with H as 32 decoded bytes. On a mismatch it does not sign.
3. On a match, the client signs one v4 extrinsic, with a mortal era, whose call is exactly `utility.batch_all([assets.transfer_keep_alive(asset, Id(payTo), amount), system.remark_with_event(R)])`, where R is the 77 ASCII bytes `lcp:sha256:` followed by H. It sends `payload.extrinsic` (the signed extrinsic) and `payload.call` (its call), both in lowercase hex, and echoes `extensions` unchanged.
4. The payer pays every fee, in DOT or in an asset its wallet selects. Nothing in this profile sponsors them.
5. A facilitator's `/verify` MUST decode the extrinsic with the network's runtime metadata; require rule 3's call, equal byte for byte to `payload.call`, for `asset`, `payTo` and `amount` exactly; require a mortal era; and require `TaggedTransactionQueue_validate_transaction` at the best block to return `Ok`. Its `/settle` MUST repeat `/verify`; submit the extrinsic; deduplicate settlements by the extrinsic's BLAKE2b-256 hash, atomically, until its era ends; and answer `success: true`, with `transaction` = `<block hash>-<extrinsic index>` and `payer` = the signer in SS58, only when the extrinsic is in a block and its events include `System.ExtrinsicSuccess`, `System.Remarked` for R and `Assets.Transferred` for `asset`. Broadcast but not yet in a block, it answers `settlement_pending` with `transaction` = the extrinsic's BLAKE2b-256 hash.
6. The resource server refuses a payment whose remark does not decode to an H that it issued for that request and has not seen claimed.
7. Declarations for x402's facilitator-submitted family: fee payer: the payer, self-funded. Replay primitive: the payer's account nonce, shared with the payer's account state, so a payer has one pending payment per nonce, and any other extrinsic the payer lands with that nonce invalidates this payment, even after the resource handler has run. Validity window: the mortal era, at most `BlockHashCount` (4096) blocks. Duplicate submission: the same bytes have the same hash, and once included their nonce is spent; settlements are still deduplicated as rule 5 requires.
8. H is on chain as the remark inside the extrinsic the payer signed, and `System.Remarked` carries its BLAKE2b-256. The extrinsic is read by block and index. No index searches remarks.
