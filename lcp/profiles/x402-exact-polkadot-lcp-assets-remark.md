**LCP profile `x402/exact/polkadot/lcp-assets-remark`: the ATR hash in a payer-signed remark, batched with the transfer.**

*Scope.* x402 v2, `scheme` `"exact"`, `network` `polkadot:68d56f15f85d3136970ec16946040bc1` (Polkadot Asset Hub) or `polkadot:67f9723393ef76214df0118c34bbbd3d` (Westend Asset Hub), `extra.assetTransferMethod` `"lcp-assets-remark"` (required). It defines this transfer method until x402 or MPP defines Polkadot. The default flow is `authorization`, and `upfront` is allowed.
1. The option gives `amount` in the asset's atomic units as the canonical decimal of a positive integer (digits only, the first not `0`), `asset` as the `pallet-assets` asset id in decimal, `payTo` in SS58, and `maxTimeoutSeconds` as an integer from 1 to 3,600. `PaymentRequired` carries `extensions.legalContext.info` = `{"type":"sha256","value":H,"legalContextUrl":L}`, where H is the SHA-256 of the ATR's exact bytes as `0x` lowercase hex and L is an `https` URL that serves them.
2. The client fetches L, computes SHA-256 over the bytes received, and compares the result with H as 32 decoded bytes. On a mismatch it does not sign.
3. On a match, the client signs one v4 extrinsic, with a mortal era, whose call is exactly `utility.batch_all([assets.transfer_keep_alive(asset, Id(payTo), amount), system.remark_with_event(R)])`, where R is the 77 ASCII bytes `lcp:sha256:` followed by H. It sends `payload.extrinsic` (the signed extrinsic) and `payload.call` (its call), both in lowercase hex, and echoes `extensions` unchanged.
4. The payer pays every fee, in DOT or in an asset its wallet selects. Nothing in this profile sponsors them.
5. A facilitator's `/verify` MUST decode the extrinsic with the network's runtime metadata; require rule 3's call, equal byte for byte to `payload.call`, for `asset`, `payTo` and `amount` exactly; require a mortal era; and require `TaggedTransactionQueue_validate_transaction` at the best block to return `Ok`. Its `/settle` MUST repeat `/verify`; submit the extrinsic; deduplicate settlements by the extrinsic's BLAKE2b-256 hash, atomically, until its era ends; and answer `success: true`, with `transaction` = `<block hash>-<extrinsic index>` and `payer` = the signer in SS58, only when the extrinsic is in a block and its events include `System.ExtrinsicSuccess`, `System.Remarked` for R and `Assets.Transferred` for `asset`. Broadcast but not yet in a block, it answers `settlement_pending` with `transaction` = the extrinsic's BLAKE2b-256 hash. Its answer's `transaction` is a hint to a reader of the payment, never the read's key (rule 8).
6. The resource server refuses a payment whose remark does not decode to an H that it issued for that request and has not seen claimed.
7. Declarations for x402's facilitator-submitted family: fee payer: the payer, self-funded. Replay primitive: the payer's account nonce, shared with the payer's account state, so a payer has one pending payment per nonce, and any other extrinsic the payer lands with that nonce invalidates this payment, even after the resource handler has run. Validity window: the mortal era, at most `BlockHashCount` (4096) blocks. Duplicate submission: the same bytes have the same hash, and once included their nonce is spent; settlements are still deduplicated as rule 5 requires.
8. H is on chain as the remark inside the extrinsic the payer signed, and `System.Remarked` carries its BLAKE2b-256. No index searches remarks. The payment is read by the signer's account nonce, never by the extrinsic's hash, since any re-signing at the same nonce has another hash; a timepoint a facilitator names is a hint. The read is the profile `read-status`'s, with these facts:
   - **The marks** are `head`, the latest block the endpoint serves, and `finalized`, the final mark; a position is a block height, and a transaction is named by its timepoint.
   - **The instrument** is the signer's account nonce. The reference names the signer, the signed nonce and the signed era, read from the bytes that follow the signature (the era, then the compact nonce, the first explicit bytes of the extensions), the presented extrinsic, and the option's payee.
   - **The search** reads every block after the position the caller gives up to the latest head, at most `POLKADOT_BOUNDS.searchBlocks` blocks a call, the next call taking the search's position. It decodes each item's preamble: a bare item, and another signer's item, is passed whatever its size; the signer's own items are held to the size bound; an item it cannot decode, which could be the signer's, leaves the search not read whole. The signer's extrinsic at the signed nonce is the instrument's, and every extrinsic whose bytes hold R is read for the search for H.
   - **Succeeded** means the extrinsic dispatched (`System.ExtrinsicSuccess`, the endpoint's `success`) with `System.Remarked` for R, wherever R sits in its call. What it moved is recorded as observed: every `Balances.Transfer` and every `Transferred` of the assets pallet's instances (`assets`, `foreignAssets`, `poolAssets`), with its asset, amount and recipient, and, where the extrinsic ends with rule 3's call of zero, which emits no event, a transfer of zero to its `dest`. Each transfer is named by the extrinsic's timepoint and its place: the index of its event among the extrinsic's events as the endpoint lists them, or, for the transfer of zero, `call 0`, the first call of rule 3's batch. On the act `payment` the extrinsic names the payee's account where one of these reaches `payTo`'s 32-byte account, whatever asset or amount; one that names no such account is not the payment. An agreement is held to no account.
   - **The answer for the instrument.** The signer's extrinsic at the signed nonce settles where it succeeded and is the act asked about; where it holds R and did not succeed (`System.ExtrinsicFailed`, or a wrapper whose failed inner call took the remark with it) and, on the act `payment`, its signed call names `payTo`'s account (a failed extrinsic's transfers being rolled back with it, the account it names is its signed call's), it is `failed`, `transaction-failed`; in every other case, one that holds no R, whatever its result, or, on the act `payment`, names no payee's account, it is `failed`, `instrument-consumed`. Each `failed` but `before-position` is answered only once the search is read whole to the latest head and found no extrinsic of the signer that is the act asked about: one it finds settles, at `head` it is `pending`, `below-mark`.
   - **The lapse.** An era signs a period and a phase and no birth block, so its end is proven only where the chain's validation of the presented extrinsic (`TaggedTransactionQueue_validate_transaction`, through the declared `json-rpc` endpoint) answered valid at a head; the last block that can include it is then the era's last for that head, and no later than `BlockHashCount` + 1 blocks after its birth. A `pending` answer keeps that head in its search position, as `validated`, and the next call takes it back. The era's end is compared with finalized alone: ended at the head and not at finalized, the extrinsic can still land, and the read is `instrument-live`. `instrument-unused` is answered once the finalized height is past that last block, the account nonce at finalized is not past the signed one, and the search is read whole. With no validation answered valid, and for an immortal era, the lapse is never answered: the read is `pending`.
   - **The payment presented.** On `settled`, `presented` is `yes` where the landed extrinsic's signed body, its signature apart, is the presented extrinsic's, and `no` otherwise.
   - **Foreign items.** An extrinsic of the range that holds R, succeeded and is not the act asked about is named with its reason: under another signer `other-signer`, signed by the payee `payee-signed`, one the caller gave `given`, and on the act `payment` one of the signer's that names no payee's account `other-account`. One that did not succeed is no foreign item.
   - **Read-only forms.** A `proxy.proxy` or `multisig.as_multi_threshold_1` wrapper and an immortal era are read and described where a payer lands one; a facilitator submits none of them (rule 5).
   - **Cases that cannot arise.** The read kit's cases that cannot arise on this ledger, each named by its reason or the opening words of its text: `not-found`, since the read holds the single-use value, the account nonce, which it reads at finalized; *an act of another pairing or kind carrying H*, since the network has one pairing and every extrinsic of the signer that carries R is its act wherever R sits, an agreement and a charge alike; *an act the read cannot place against the position given* (its three cases) and *an item the search could not decode that gives no position*, since every extrinsic is read at its block's height; *the validity passed with the value unconsumed, read below the final mark*, since the era's end is compared with finalized alone; and *a read with no means to read the single-use value* and *a read that did not read the value at the latest head*, since the read holds the account nonce and reads it at finalized and at the latest head.
9. **The agreement.** The payment form is also offered as an agreement, at the floor: 1 base unit, since the assets pallet returns early on a transfer of zero and emits no `Transferred` event, which rule 5 requires. The signer pays every fee (rule 4); no other party can pay on Polkadot Asset Hub. The extrinsic must succeed (rule 8); an extrinsic the option's payee signed is no agreement and no payment, on either act. The payee must already hold the asset, since a transfer of one unit cannot open an asset account below the asset's minimum balance; no validation reads it.
