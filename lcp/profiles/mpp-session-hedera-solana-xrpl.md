**LCP profile `mpp/session/hedera-solana-xrpl`: the ATR hash at a session's opening, or, on the Hedera, Solana and XRPL stock sessions, an agreement recorded before it.** Clauses 1–3 and 5 of `mpp/charge` apply to the opening challenge. One ATR covers the whole channel.

1. `hedera`: the stock session, `mpp/session/hedera/stock`, at coverage `none`. The stock client sends `approve` and the escrow's `open` itself, with a random `salt`, so nothing the payer signs carries H: the session is served only after an agreement on its own network is recorded, and its opening counts for the sale only where it comes after that agreement and the account that opened it signed the agreement, which the server compares. `pairingsOf` names it for a `session` challenge on `hedera` that names no `channelId`, whose `chainId` is absent, 295 or 296, and whose `currency`, `recipient` and `escrowContract` are addresses; `bound` finds no signed place for H. `reference` takes the read keys from the opening's credential and the payee from the `recipient` of the challenge it is given, and refuses one that is not, by the members fixed at issue, the challenge the credential echoes, `mpp/challenge-not-option`. The read (`status`, under the profile `read-status`) takes the opening by the `txHash` its credential names, through the network's JSON-RPC relay, at one mark, `finalized`, every block the relay serves being final; a network that declares no relay is not served. A revert is read with the mirror's contract result for the hash (`GET /api/v1/contracts/results/{transactionIdOrHash}`, its `result`), since on Hedera only a transaction executed in the EVM takes the sender nonce it signed, one that failed the node's validations before the EVM leaving it as it was (HIP-844, *Rationale*). A revert executed in the EVM, `CONTRACT_REVERT_EXECUTED`, is `transaction-failed` where the call its sender signed is the escrow's `open` to the challenge's `recipient`, and `instrument-consumed` otherwise, a revert moving nothing. Any other failed result, or none on the mirror, shows no nonce taken, a status such as `INSUFFICIENT_GAS` coming both before and in the EVM (HIP-844, *Backward Compatibility*), and the same signed bytes can still land under the same hash: the read, holding no nonce state, answers it `pending`, `search-incomplete`, never `failed`, at or before the position given as after it. The mirror's REST interface serves no signer nonce after a transaction, its contract result naming only the `result`, the `status` and the wrapped transaction's own `nonce`. The cost, stated: an opening that failed in the EVM under another status, out of gas among them, is answered `pending` as one refused before the EVM is, never `failed`. A receipt holding exactly one `ChannelOpened` from the escrow naming the channel, whose members give the channel id it names, is the opening: its signer is the account id the mirror resolves the log's `payer` to (`GET /api/v1/accounts/{address}`), an address it resolves to no account being another account; it is the payee's own act where that is the account the `recipient` resolves to; it names the payee where the log's `payee` is the `recipient` or resolves to the same account; and what moved is the deposit, its beneficiary the log's `payee`, as the account id the mirror resolves it to where it resolves to one, named by the transaction and the log's place among the receipt's logs, its token and amount compared with nothing. What was presented is the transaction the credential names, so a `settled` answer, which names it, states `presented` `yes`. Any other receipt that succeeded is no opening of the channel, `instrument-consumed`. No receipt is `pending`, `not-found`, where the mirror holds the blocks after the position given, by the number of its oldest block or an archival declaration, and `unreadable`, `history-gap`, otherwise. Declared inapplicable to the read kit: `below-mark`, every block the relay serves being final; `instrument-live`, `instrument-unused` and `search-incomplete`, the read reading no nonce state, making no search and stating no lapse, the one case of `search-incomplete` at coverage `none` being a search's item it could not decode; a revert executed in the EVM arises, and any other failed opening answers `pending`, `search-incomplete`.
2. `solana`, `@solana/mpp` 0.7.0's session, its members in the request itself (`src/Methods.ts:258-301`), as two pairings. A challenge whose `modes` is exactly `["pull"]` and whose `pullVoucherStrategy` is `clientVoucher` is `mpp/session/solana/memo`: the stock client opens in the first mode listed and under it composes an `open` whose fee payer and rent payer is the challenge's `operator`, the seller's key (`src/client/Session.ts:767`; `src/client/PaymentChannels.ts:104-173`, `:198-215`), so the buyer's client cannot broadcast it alone, and the seller's server, set to `openTxSubmitter: "server"`, co-signs and submits it (`src/server/Session.ts:110`, `:472-485`). lcp's client composes that stock `open` and adds one Memo Program instruction whose data is H's LCP string, `lcp:sha256:0x` and 64 lowercase hex digits; it states the composed transaction's size, its two signatures included, and refuses one past the 1,232-byte packet before signing (`svm/tx-too-large`). Before the server submits anything it refuses an open action that carries no `transaction`, which the stock server-opened opener writes (`src/client/PaymentChannels.ts:254-263`), and an `open` without the memo, a stock `open` among them (`svm/no-carrier`); an `open` whose fee payer or rent payer is not the `operator`, or whose payer is (`svm/open-not-operator`); and a memo spelled otherwise (`svm/carrier-not-canonical`), more than one (`svm/memo-count`), or one naming another H than the challenge's id (`mpp/carrier-not-challenge`). The stock server's check of the `open` finds the payment-channels instruction among the transaction's instructions and refuses no other (`src/server/session/on-chain.ts:569-581`). A challenge whose `pullVoucherStrategy` is `operatedVoucher` is refused at `pairingsOf`, `svm/operated-voucher`: its stock opening is a token delegation, which deposits nothing and may broadcast nothing (`src/client/Session.ts:769-777`; `src/server/Session.ts:524-548`). Under any other challenge the buyer's client broadcasts the stock `open`, whose salt is a random number, and the pairing is `mpp/session/solana/stock`, at coverage `none` with no link: the agreement is made first, on the session's network, and the opening counts only where it comes after the agreement was recorded and the account that opened it signed the agreement, that account being the `open`'s `payer`, account 0, whose token account funds the deposit (`src/client/Session.ts:795`; `src/client/PaymentChannels.ts:153`), never its `authorizedSigner`, the session key that signs vouchers. Its `bound` answers `mpp/no-signed-place`, and the read names the opening by its signature. Where a client writes the session bearer proof of draft-solana-session-00 (`sessionProof`), its `sessionChallengeId` is the opening challenge's `id`; `@solana/mpp` 0.7.0 writes none. Their read: `mpp/session/solana/memo`'s is the one `x402/upto/solana`'s profile states, its act the opening, its deposit counting where the distribution its `open` names holds the option's payee; `mpp/session/solana/stock`'s, at coverage `none`, looks up the opening named by its signature at `finalized` and at the latest head, `confirmed`, which is no mark, and makes no search, the opening being the act whether or not it carries H. Declared inapplicable to the read kit, cases the read cannot meet on Solana: on both, the case settled at a finality asked for below the final mark, since a seller names `finalized` alone; on the memo session, while the search proves no absence, every case at coverage `full` whose answer is `instrument-unused`, `instrument-consumed` or `transaction-failed`, and each transfer of what moved is named by its transaction and its place in it, since its act is an opening that holds exactly one `open`, whose one deposit into the channel's token account is what moved, a transaction holding more being no act of this pairing; and, both reads holding the signed message, a read that did not read the value at the latest head and a value whose validity passed, unconsumed at the final mark and consumed at the latest head.
3. `xrpl`, two pairings. `mpp/session/xrpl`, the in-band opening, at coverage `full`: the `PaymentChannelCreate`, which the payer signs, single-signed or multisigned, and the seller's server submits, carries exactly one memo whose `MemoData` is the UTF-8 of `lcp:sha256:` followed by H (none, `xrpl/no-memo`; more than one, or more than 8 memos, `xrpl/memo-count`), deposits XRP, the only currency of an XRPL session (`xrpl/currency-not-xrp`), and carries no `NetworkID`, as rippled's `preflight0` requires on each network the challenge names, whose ids are at most 1024 (a blob that carries one is refused `xrpl/network-mismatch`); a stock client writes no memo, and its opening is refused before the server submits it. `mpp/session/xrpl/stock`, the stock session, at coverage `none`: the stock client opens the channel itself, out of band, with no memo, so nothing the payer signs carries H, and the session is served only after an agreement on its own network is recorded; its credentials are vouchers and the close, each naming its channel and its payer by `source`, `did:pkh:xrpl:{network}:{address}`, and an `open` credential, which the server would submit with no memo, is refused before anything is submitted (`mpp/session-action`). Each pairing's `reference` refuses a credential whose echoed challenge is not the challenge it is given (`mpp/challenge-not-option`). The read (`status`, under the profile `read-status`) of the in-band opening is that of the profile `x402/exact/xrpl`, rules 9 to 15, by the create's `Sequence` or ticket, with the one LCP memo as the carrier and the create's `Amount`, XRP, as what moved, one deposit per transaction, its `Destination` the channel's beneficiary: one mark, `validated`, and, until a run shows that `account_tx` lists every transaction of the payer's account, no `failed` answer but `before-position`. The stock session's read takes the channel the voucher names: its `PayChannel` at the validated ledger (`ledger_entry`), then the `PaymentChannelCreate` whose metadata creates that entry, in the `account_tx` of the channel's `Account`, or, where the validated ledger does not show the channel, of the payer `source` names, from the ledger the create must come after to the latest ledger the server serves. That create is the act where its `Account` is the payer and its `Destination` the payee, `presented` `yes`; one another account opened, or one to another account, is `failed`, `instrument-consumed`; one found at or before the position, `failed`, `before-position`. A channel the validated ledger shows whose create the search, read whole, does not hold was created at or before the position only if `account_tx` lists every transaction of the account, a delegated create and an inner `Batch` create among them, which no run has yet shown: until one does, the read answers `pending`, `search-incomplete`, the search's position held where it began, where it would answer `failed`, `before-position`; on a server whose `complete_ledgers` does not hold the range, or that refuses `account_tx` for ledgers it does not hold (`x402/exact/xrpl`, rule 14), `unreadable`, `history-gap`, never absence. The cost: until that run a channel created at or before the position whose create the search does not find holds its leg to its hold. A channel the validated ledger does not show whose create the search, read whole, does not hold is `pending`, `not-found`. The seller's server broadcasts every payment the stock session takes: it redeems the vouchers and submits the close's `PaymentChannelClaim` from the recipient's wallet (`xrpl-mpp-sdk` 0.1.0-beta.4, `dist/channel/server/index.js:686-697`), the opening being no credential of the pairing, so its `broadcaster` answers `payee`. A reported close of the in-band session is read by the transaction it names (`tx`): `settled` where its metadata deletes the channel, and it tests no party, the close being the payee's own transaction. Declared inapplicable to the read kit: on both, *an act read at the finality asked for, below the final mark*, since a seller names `validated` alone, and *each transfer of what moved is named by its transaction and its place in it*, since a `PaymentChannelCreate` makes one deposit; on the in-band opening, `not-found`, since the payment carries the signed transaction, so the read holds its sequence number or ticket, *a payment act carrying H that transferred zero* and *an act carrying H that transferred zero*, since a `PaymentChannelCreate` of zero is malformed, and, while the search proves no absence, each case at coverage `full` whose answer on an act is `instrument-unused`, `instrument-consumed` or `transaction-failed`, which the search's gate holds, the vectors holding the read's answer, `pending`, `search-incomplete`; on the stock session, `instrument-live`, `instrument-unused` and `transaction-failed`, since a create whose metadata creates the channel's entry has landed and succeeded.
4. On `solana`'s memo session and on `xrpl`, the server refuses an opening that lacks its carrier, before it broadcasts where the method lets it.
