**LCP profile `mpp/charge/xrpl`: the ATR hash as the charge Payment's `InvoiceID`.** Rules 1–3 and 5 of `mpp/charge` apply to the challenge.

1. The request's `methodDetails.invoiceId` is H's 32 bytes as 64 uppercase hex digits. A request whose `invoiceId` is present with another value is refused `xrpl/carrier-occupied`: the `Payment` has one `InvoiceID`, and a seller's own reference belongs in the ATR's content.
2. The request is the stock `xrpl-mpp-sdk` 0.1.0-beta.4 charge request (`dist/chunk-L6LVAN2P.js:17-90`). `methodDetails.network` names `mainnet`, `testnet` or `devnet`, the networks `xrpl:0`, `xrpl:1` and `xrpl:2`; it has no default (`xrpl/network-missing`, else `xrpl/network-malformed`). `currency` is a string, as the stock schema has it: `XRP`, an issued currency as `CUR:issuer` or as the JSON text of `{"currency","issuer"}`, or an MPT as the JSON text of `{"mpt_issuance_id"}` (`dist/chunk-JQG7ATQA.js:170-183`); an object is refused `mpp/request-malformed`. lcp reads these forms whole, where the stock `parseCurrency` reads the first two parts of a `CUR:issuer` text with more colons and the two members of a JSON text that holds others: a text with a second colon, or a JSON text with any other member, is refused `mpp/request-malformed` before anything moves, lcp's own stricter reading, so that no part of the currency the challenge names goes unread. `amount` is drops for XRP and the value otherwise. `methodDetails.memos` is absent, or an array of objects whose only members are the optional strings `type`, `format` and `data`; `destinationTag` and `sourceTag` are numbers where present.
3. The payer signs one `Payment`, as the stock client writes it (`dist/client/index.js:333-346`): `InvoiceID` the request's `invoiceId`, which is H; `Destination` the request's `recipient`, with its `destinationTag`; `Amount` from `currency` as the stock `buildAmount` writes it, drops, an issued amount, or an MPT amount, where lcp's build writes an issued amount's `SendMax` equal to it and the stock client computes `SendMax` from the issuer's transfer rate and its slippage and finds paths (`dist/client/index.js:42-104`), so that lcp's build fails with a `tec` result where the issuer charges a transfer fee; `SourceTag` the request's `sourceTag`, or 593184257, the stock `MPP_SOURCE_TAG`; `Memos` from `memos` where it holds any, each member's UTF-8 in uppercase hex (`:391-399`); a `LastLedgerSequence`; and no `NetworkID`, each network the challenge can name having an id of at most 1024. It may be single-signed or multisigned.
4. The credential names its payer by `source`, `did:pkh:xrpl:{network}:{address}`, the network as `methodDetails.network` names it, as the stock client writes it and the stock server reads and requires it before anything else. Its payload is `{"type":"transaction","blob"}`, the signed blob the seller's server submits (pull), or `{"type":"hash","hash"}`, a transaction the payer broadcast itself (push), which no challenge member excludes, the mode being the stock client's own setting. A `hash` credential is read by its hash once the transaction is in a validated ledger (`fetchPresented`), and only then checked.
5. The server refuses a credential whose signed `InvoiceID` is not the request's `invoiceId` and H (`xrpl/carrier-mismatch`), whose blob carries a `NetworkID` (`xrpl/network-mismatch`), whose `transaction` payload's blob is over 8,192 hex digits, 4,096 bytes, the most the stock server's schema takes (`dist/chunk-L6LVAN2P.js:16`, `:24`), a large multisigned quorum's among them (`xrpl/blob-too-large`), or whose blob is over 16,384 bytes, lcp's bound on any XRPL blob it decodes (`xrpl/blob-too-large`), or whose signed transaction is not a `Payment` (`xrpl/not-payment`), before anything moves; rule 10 of `mpp/charge` orders the checks, the server's own record that this H's act is now taken, and then the stock server's verification and broadcast.

**The reference.**

6. `reference` gives the network, the transaction's hash, H, the payee (the `recipient` of the challenge it is given, the key of the two party tests), and, for a `transaction` credential, the payer's instrument as signed, as the profile `x402/exact/xrpl` rule 7 states it; a `hash` credential gives the transaction it names alone, and the read finds the instrument from it. It refuses a credential whose echoed challenge is not that challenge, compared by RFC 8785 form (`mpp/challenge-not-option`). A payment of this pairing is never an agreement.

**The read.**

7. The read is that of the profile `x402/exact/xrpl`, rules 9 to 15, with MPP's `InvoiceID`, H itself, as the carrier: one mark, `validated`; found by the payer's instrument; what moved, the signer and the authority; the lapse; the search for H through the payer's `account_tx`; the history; and, until a run shows that `account_tx` lists every transaction of the payer's account, no `failed` answer but `before-position`, the read answering `pending`, `search-incomplete`. A `Payment` that took the instrument and paid another account than the payee's is `instrument-consumed` (read-status rule 9) once the gate is lifted.
8. A payment presented by its hash alone is first looked up by that hash (`tx`) from the issue ledger to the validated ledger; once found it names the instrument, and the read is rule 7's, `presented` `yes` where the act is the transaction the credential names and `no` where it is another. Where the lookup is read whole and finds nothing, the answer is `pending`, `not-found`: the read holds no sequence number or ticket for that payment.

**The read kit.**

9. Declared inapplicable to the read kit: *an act read at the finality asked for, below the final mark*, since a seller names `validated` alone; *each transfer of what moved is named by its transaction and its place in it*, since each transaction the read takes holds one transfer, a `Payment` delivering one amount to its one `Destination`; *a payment act carrying H that transferred zero* and *an act carrying H that transferred zero*, since a `Payment` of zero is malformed and a partial payment that delivers nothing does not succeed; and, while the search proves no absence (rule 7), each case at coverage `full` whose answer on an act is `instrument-unused`, `instrument-consumed` or `transaction-failed`, which the search's gate holds: the vectors hold the read's answer to such a case, `pending`, `search-incomplete`. A case is named by its reason, or by the opening words of its text in `read-status.json`, and is made in the ledger's own spelling as the profile `x402/exact/xrpl` rule 16 states.
10. What a record of this pairing proves: the payer signed an XRPL `Payment` whose `InvoiceID` is this ATR's hash, and it is in a validated ledger with `tesSUCCESS`: it paid the amount `meta.delivered_amount` states to the account its `Destination` names. This does not show that amount, destination, asset or timing match the ATR's content.
