**LCP profile `mpp/session/evm-tempo`: the ATR hash in an MPP session or subscription opening.**

1. The challenge carries H as `mpp/charge` rules 1, 3 and 5 say. The id is rule 2's, except that a Tempo `subscription` challenge's id is exactly the base64url (no padding) of H.
2. Session (`evm`, `tempo`): the client opens a new channel whose `salt` is H's 32 bytes. EVM `authorization`: the EIP-3009 nonce is keccak256(abi.encode(from, recipient, currency, H, authorizedSigner)), as MPP requires. EVM `permit2`: the witness `salt` is H. The challenge lcp places for an EVM session names `credentialTypes`, without `hash`, since an absent member admits `hash`: an opening funded by a `hash`, a transaction the buyer's client broadcasts, is no part of `mpp/session/evm`, which does not offer it; the opening is an `authorization` or a `permit2` the resource server's settler submits. Tempo: the signed `open` call's salt is H, and a Tempo v2 descriptor carries it. Where the seller pays the fee, a stock signer sends the opening as the `0x78` envelope `mpp/charge/tempo/split-memo` rule 6 states, read as the `0x76` form its sender signed.
3. A session challenge naming `channelId` resumes a channel. The client pays it with a voucher only for a channel it opened for an ATR it compared.
4. Subscription (`tempo`): the key authorization's `witness` is the id's 32 bytes, which is H.
5. One ATR covers the whole session or subscription. Vouchers, top-ups and renewals carry no new ATR.
6. **The read of an opening** (`read-status`). The opening is found by the escrow's `ChannelOpened` naming the channel id, under each session version's event: on v2 the TIP-20 channel reserve's, its salt data word 3; on the v1 escrow `ChannelOpened(bytes32 channelId, address payer, address payee, address token, address authorizedSigner, bytes32 salt, uint256 deposit)`, the first three indexed, topic `0xcd6e60364f8ee4c2b0d62afc07a1fb04fd267ce94693f93f8f85daaa099b5c94`, its salt data word 2; the salt is H. On EVM the escrow emits no event, and the opening is found by its funding: an `authorization` by the currency's `AuthorizationUsed` of the payer and the nonce, a `permit2` by every contract's `Transfer` from the payer to the escrow, each transaction decoded for the escrow's `openWithAuthorization` or `openWithPermit2` call, whose salt is H; an `open` the payer sends itself, funded by a `hash`, is an act of another kind. A decoded call counts only where the receipt shows the escrow ran it: an `openWithAuthorization` by its token's `AuthorizationUsed` of its `from` and the nonce the escrow recomputes over the call's from, payee, token, salt and authorized signer, an `openWithPermit2` or an `open` only as the transaction's own call to the escrow, each with its deposit's `Transfer` to the escrow; a call carrying H or the funding's nonce that the receipt does not show so makes the transaction an item the read cannot decode, which holds the search, so a `permit2` opening sent through a batch is never counted. A value read consumed at the position given whose consumer the search did not meet is found in the first block it reads consumed at, by halving from block 0, by the funding's own logs there. Its value is the funding's, `authorizationState(payer, nonce)` on the currency, or the payer's Permit2 bit at the nonce it signed, one across its tokens, read at `finalized` and at `latest`. The deposit goes to the escrow, whose channel `payee` and token are recorded as the beneficiary and the asset the opening names: the opening pays where that `payee` is the challenge's `recipient`, the token compared with nothing. The payer is the sender of the Tempo transaction, in its `0x76` form whatever envelope carried it, or the account an EVM opening's authorization or permit is signed by. The payment presented is, on Tempo, the fields the sender signed, compared by the hash `expiringNonceHash` computes over them, and on EVM the authorization or permit the payer signed. The channel id is a digest over the payee and the token as well as H, and an `authorization` opening's nonce one over the recipient and the token as well, so neither search is a search for H: a session's read answers no `failed` but `before-position`, and where its single-use value would answer `failed` it answers `pending`, `search-incomplete`. So `instrument-unused`, `instrument-consumed` and `transaction-failed` cannot arise on `mpp/session/tempo` or `mpp/session/evm` (`read-status` rule 28). On Tempo the opening's value is its transaction's nonce, read by its nonce key as `mpp/charge/tempo/split-memo` rule 8 reads it, at `finalized` and at `latest`, each at the last block below the opening's `validBefore` where that head is past it; a value read consumed whose transaction the search did not meet is found in the first block it reads consumed at. Marks: on EVM chains `latest`, `safe` and `finalized`, the final one `finalized`; on Tempo one, `finalized`, the head being final, `status` taking no other finality, and an opening above it `pending`, `below-mark`.
7. **The read of an activation** (`mpp/subscription/tempo`). The activation is found by the account keychain's `KeyAuthorizationWitness` whose account topic is the payer's account, the one whose root key signed the key authorization, and whose witness topic is H; the search reads that event's logs carrying H from the position given to the latest head, the account topic left open, and a witness log under another account is a foreign item, since H is public and any account can authorize a key with it. The activation names its recipient, the one account the key authorization the landed transaction carries lets the key pay; one that names none, or several, or allows any, a call scope with no selector rules among them, names no account. It pays where that account is the option's payee, whatever moved. A key authorization for another key than the challenge's is an act of another kind. Every token transfer from the payer's account in the same transaction, the `currency` transfer among them, is recorded as what moved, a transfer the receive-policy guard held as moved to the guard, nothing where there is none. The payment presented is the signed key authorization, compared whole with the one the landed transaction carries. A transaction whose signed bytes the endpoint does not return, or that do not decode, is an item the search could not decode. The read holds a single-use value, the key slot the presented key authorization names in the payer's keychain: `getKey(account, keyId)` on the keychain through `eth_call`, read at `finalized` and at `latest`, consumed where it names the key or reads it revoked. The keychain registers a key id for an account once: it refuses a key id it holds or has revoked, and a key authorization whose `expiry` is not after the block's timestamp (tempoxyz/tempo@c8b89f9b `crates/precompiles/src/account_keychain/mod.rs:316-334`), and keeps a revoked key's entry as revoked (`:484-490`); a transaction whose key authorization it refuses is invalid and not included (`crates/evm/src/handler.rs:925-933`). It registers the key before the transaction's calls run and outside their checkpoint, so a reverted activation registers it (`crates/evm/src/handler.rs:831-834`, `:1270-1272`), and the slot shows an activation whatever its result. A slot read consumed whose transaction the search did not meet is found in the first block at which it reads consumed, by reading the slot at blocks halving the range from the position given, or, where it reads consumed at the position given, from block 0, and in that block's signed transactions as the payer's whose key authorization names the key id or whose own call to the keychain authorizes it (`authorizeKey`, `authorizeAdminKey`; such a call counts only where its transaction succeeded); where none is found, a transaction of that block the read cannot decode holds the search. The transaction so found is read whole, its receipt deciding: a reverted activation whose key authorization carries H and names the option's payee is `failed`, `transaction-failed`; any other that consumed the slot `instrument-consumed`; one at or before the position given `before-position`. The slot's bound is the key authorization's `expiry`, where it signs one: the keychain never clears a slot and registers no key whose expiry is not after the block's timestamp, so a slot unconsumed at a head past `expiry` is never consumed by the presented key authorization, `instrument-unused` once the search is read whole; one that signs none has no bound and is never `instrument-unused`. The presented key authorization can also be cancelled without its slot: the payer's account can burn a witness (`burnKeyAuthorizationWitness`, `crates/precompiles/src/account_keychain/mod.rs:457-465`), after which the keychain refuses every key authorization of that account carrying it, and a transaction carrying one is invalid and not included (`:1198-1221`; `crates/evm/src/handler.rs:925-933`). So the read's single-use value is the slot together with H's burn mark for the payer's account, `isKeyAuthorizationWitnessBurned(account, H)` on the keychain (`crates/contracts/src/precompiles/account_keychain.rs:214-215`), read at `finalized` and at `latest` beside the slot, the value consumed where either reads consumed. A burn mark read burned whose transaction the search did not meet is found in the first block at which it reads burned, located as the slot's consumer is, by the keychain's `KeyAuthorizationWitnessBurned` log there naming the account and H (`:106-107`): it consumed the value, so the answer is `failed`, `instrument-consumed`, naming the burn, which carries H, as of another kind, and one at or before the position given is `before-position`; where that block holds no such log, the search is held, `search-incomplete`. So the search for H needs to meet only the acts carrying H that succeeded, whose receipts keep their logs: a failed one under another key consumed none of this value. Each read of the slot or the burn mark needs the endpoint to hold that block's state, one that does not answering `unreadable`, `history-gap`; the read costs two reads of the slot and two of the burn mark, and one for each halving where the slot reads consumed, or the mark burned, and its transaction was not met.
8. **The read kit's cases** (`read-status` rule 28) that cannot arise. On `mpp/session/tempo`: `not-found`, since the read holds what the payer signed, its opening's transaction, and reads that value; *an act read at the finality asked for, below the final mark*, since no finality below `finalized`, Tempo's one mark, is asked; `instrument-unused`, `instrument-consumed` and `transaction-failed`, since it answers no `failed` but `before-position` while no search for an opening is stated (rule 6); by the case's words, *an act the read cannot place against the position given* (its three cases) and *an item the search could not decode that gives no position*, since every log and transaction the read meets is in a block, which places it; *a read that did not read the value at the latest head*, since it reads the value at `latest` whenever it reads it at `finalized`; and *each transfer of what moved is named by its transaction and its place* and *an act carrying H that names no account*, since an opening moves one deposit, from the payer to the escrow, and names its channel's `payee`. On `mpp/session/evm`: `not-found`, *a read with no means to read the single-use value* and, since it states no search, *search-incomplete is answered before not-found*, the three `failed` reasons, the three unplaceable acts, the undecodable item with no position, *a read that did not read the value at the latest head* and the one deposit's two cases, for the same reasons. *An act the option's payee signed*, *an act carrying H under another account's signature*, *a transaction for which two reasons hold* and *the settled act's answer names every foreign item* arise on it: each open call the receipt shows run in a transaction its search meets is read with its own signer, the authorization's `from` or the Permit2 owner, never the transaction's sender, so an opening carrying H that the payee or another account funded beside the payer's funding is a foreign item. On `mpp/subscription/tempo`: `not-found`, *a read with no means to read the single-use value* and *a read that did not read the value at the latest head*, since its read holds its single-use value, the key slot the presented key authorization names, and reads it at `finalized` and at `latest` (rule 7); *an act carrying H that names no account and consumed nothing*, *an act the caller gave as another act under the same H*, *transactions carrying H with a failed result, the payer's own and one the payee signed* and the two cases opening *a failed result that left the value unconsumed*, whose facts each give an act of the payer's of the pairing's kind that consumed nothing, since an act is of that kind only where its key authorization names the issued key id, and the keychain registers that key's slot whatever the transaction's result, a transaction whose key authorization it refuses being not included, so every such act consumed the value; the payer's key authorization for another key id that carries H consumes nothing of the slot and is an act of another kind, met by the search where it succeeded and named `other-kind`; *an act read at the finality asked for, below the final mark*, since no finality below `finalized`, Tempo's one mark, is asked; and the three unplaceable acts and the undecodable item with no position, since every log and transaction the read meets is in a block, which places it.
