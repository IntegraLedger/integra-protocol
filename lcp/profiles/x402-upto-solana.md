**LCP profile `x402/upto/solana`: the ATR hash as the Memo instruction of the `open` that escrows the signed maximum.** x402's `upto` scheme on Solana, a one-request payment channel (`@x402/svm` 2.30.0).

1. The option's `extra.memo` is `lcp:sha256:0x` + 64 lowercase hex of H, 77 UTF-8 bytes, which the stock client writes as one Memo Program instruction of the `open` transaction the buyer signs (`@x402/svm` 2.30.0, `src/upto/client/scheme.ts:88`). `bound` holds the presented `accepted` to the option filter and refuses a transaction unless exactly one of its top-level Memo instructions holds an LCP string and it is H's, the only Memo instruction, or whose payment-channels instruction is no `open` (`x402/not-an-opening`): no LCP-form memo is `svm/no-carrier`, more than one `svm/memo-count`, one spelled otherwise `svm/carrier-not-canonical`, one other than `extra.memo` `svm/carrier-mismatch`; a durable nonce account loaded from an address table is `svm/nonce-account-not-static`. `reference` names the message's digest, the buyer, the fee payer, H, the option's payee and the blockhash or durable nonce, and refuses a payment whose `accepted` is not the option it is given, `x402/accepted-not-option`.
2. The settler is the fee payer and the rent payer, and broadcasts the `open`. The amount charged is named later by the seller's server, which sets it in the requirements it sends its settler; that settlement carries no H and is not read, and the sale's amount is the offer's, the maximum the `open` escrows.
3. The client builds the highest message version both `extra.transactionVersions` and lcp admit: version 1 where the list holds 1, version 0 where it holds 0 alone or is absent, as a stock client does (`@x402/svm` at `88b66dd4`, `utils.ts:200-203`); a list that admits neither is refused at the filter (`x402/option-malformed`). `bound` and `reference` decode legacy, version 0 and version 1 messages, and refuse one whose version the list does not admit, `svm/version-not-admitted`, `legacy` being admitted where the list holds 0 and the list absent being `[0]`, as `@x402/svm` 2.31.0's facilitator admits it (`isAcceptedTransactionVersion`): a payment the option does not admit is no answer to it.
4. An option carrying `extra.splits` is refused at the filter, `svm/input-malformed`, before the buyer signs: no Solana build writes split legs, and each pays the whole amount to `payTo`.
5. The read, `status`, as the profile `read-status` answers it: one mark, `finalized`, the default and the final mark. The search for H lists the memo-carrying signatures of the buyer's own account, which every transaction the buyer signs loads, never the fee payer's, from the issue slot to the latest head, `confirmed`, which is no mark: 10 pages of 1,000 and 50 candidates a pass, a pass that meets either bound, or a candidate no lookup finds or one that does not decode, holding a handle the next read resumes from, below the pages it read, with the transactions met that the answer rests on, a consumer of the value or one the payer signed, other than those the caller gave, which the next read looks up by signature. An act met only above the `finalized` slot is `pending`, `below-mark`, named with no finality. The parties come from the landed transaction: who signed it, and the distribution the `open` names, its deposit counting where that distribution holds the option's payee and recorded with that payee as its beneficiary, or the distribution's first recipient where it does not hold the payee; each transfer of what moved is named by the transaction and its instruction's place, the top-level index or `<index>.<place>` for an inner instruction, and a transaction with a failed result names the destination the payer signed and moved nothing. `presented` compares the landed message with the one presented, by its digest, or with the transaction presented alone. The single-use value is the signed message: consumed by its own landing or by its durable nonce advanced; unused once its blockhash has lapsed, `isBlockhashValid` answering `false` at `finalized` at a `finalized` slot at least 150 past the context slot of the first `isBlockhashValid` answer of `true` at `confirmed`, which the search's handle keeps, a slot count alone deciding nothing, since a skipped slot adds no block and the chain ends the blockhash's validity 150 blocks after its own, and a blockhash read invalid before such an answer deciding nothing; a durable-nonce message never lapses. Until a run shows every transaction the buyer signs listed under its account, a search read whole proves no absence: no `failed` but `before-position` is answered, `pending`, `search-incomplete` in its place.
6. Declared inapplicable to the read kit, cases the read cannot meet on Solana: the case settled at a finality asked for below the final mark, since a seller names `finalized` alone; while the search proves no absence, every case at coverage `full` whose answer is `instrument-unused`, `instrument-consumed` or `transaction-failed`, which the read answers `pending`, `search-incomplete` (`vectors/svm-read.json`); each transfer of what moved is named by its transaction and its place in it, since the act is an opening that holds exactly one `open`, whose one deposit into the channel's token account is what moved, a transaction holding more being no act of this pairing; and, the read holding the signed message, a read that did not read the value at the latest head, since its search and its lookups read on to `confirmed`, and a value whose validity passed, unconsumed at the final mark and consumed at the latest head, since a blockhash message is consumed only by its own transaction, which that read finds, and a durable-nonce message has no lapse.
7. The payment-channels program is on devnet only: on mainnet the pairing waits until a read shows the program deployed there. It is never an agreement option.
8. What a record of this pairing proves: the payer's own signed act carries this ATR's hash: a Solana transaction the buyer's account signed, whose one Memo instruction is the hash's LCP string, final at `finalized` with no error, which deposited the amount its `open` names into a payment channel whose distribution names the option's payee, recorded with its mint and amount. The memo is in the transaction's instruction data on chain. The deposit is the signed maximum a one-request channel escrows; the amount charged from it, which may be zero, is the seller's metering, settled in a later transaction that does not carry the hash, and the rest returns to the payer. It compares no amount, mint or time with the ATR's terms.
