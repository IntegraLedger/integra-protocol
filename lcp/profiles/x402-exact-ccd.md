**LCP profile `x402/exact/ccd`: the ATR hash as the transfer memo.**

**Status.** This profile defines the pairing `x402/exact/ccd`. Its `confirmed` is `no`: nothing outside lcp has yet shown a payment of this form with H as its memo. Its `nonce` is `unshown` and its data holds no `agreement` (rule 7). Until a recorded run shows the form, it is not offered.

1. The `PaymentRequired` carries `extensions.legalContext.info` = `{"type":"sha256","value":H,"legalContextUrl":L}`, H the SHA-256 of the ATR's exact bytes as `0x` lowercase hex and L an `https` URL serving them.
2. The client fetches L, hashes the bytes received, and compares with H as 32 bytes. On a mismatch it does not sign.
3. On a match its one transfer carries a memo: for CCD (the option's `asset` read without regard to case, as the stock facilitator reads it), `SimpleTransferWithMemo` whose memo is the CBOR text string `lcp:sha256:` + H; for a PLT, a `transfer` operation whose `memo` is CBOR tag 24 around those bytes. The transaction is the sponsored form, version 1: the header the sender signs names the sender, its sequence number, the `expiry` and the sponsor, the option's `extra.feePayer`. The client sets the expiry as the stock client does, the time it signs plus `maxTimeoutSeconds` less 5 seconds. The option's `network` is `ccd:` and the first 32 lowercase hex digits of the genesis block hash (CAIP-2). Its `amount` may be `"0"`: the scheme asks strict equality and sets no floor.
4. The server refuses a payment whose memo does not carry an H it issued for this request and has not seen claimed.
5. The memo is in the finalized `AccountTransfer` or `TokenTransfer` event of the settlement transaction.

**The settler and the agreement**

6. The settler, as sponsor, adds its signature and submits what the sender signed, and pays the fee, always. The stock facilitator checks the sponsor against `extra.feePayer`, the receiver against `payTo`, the amount by equality, the expiry against `maxTimeoutSeconds`, the sender's signature and that the signed sequence number is the account's next one; it reads no memo, so the check of rule 4 is the server's, made before it calls the settler. The transaction it names is a hint: the read of rule 9 decides.
7. The pairing's payment can itself be an agreement once its single use is shown: its floor is zero; the fee is the sponsor's; the item must be finalized with a successful outcome; and an act whose sender is the option's payee, compared as an account, is no agreement and no payment. A `rejected` outcome is included, charged its fee, and consumes the sender's sequence number: it is no agreement. Until a run shows a landed payment refused when sent again, the pairing's `nonce` is `unshown` and its data holds no `agreement`: the agreement option waits on that run.

**The read** (the profile `read-status`)

8. `reference` gives the network, H, the header's sender (its base58check text), the signed sequence number (a decimal string), the signed `expiry` in seconds (the payment's deadline), the option's `payTo` for the two party tests, and the digest of what the sender signed (rule 14). It refuses a header whose sequence number, `numSignatures` or sponsor's `numSignatures` is not a positive integer, whose `executionEnergyAmount` is not a non-negative one, that names no sponsor, or whose sponsor is not an account address, `ccd/transaction-malformed` or `ccd/address-malformed`, and a payment whose `accepted` is not the option it is given, compared as RFC 8785 JSON, `x402/accepted-not-option`. It names no asset: nothing the transfer moved is compared with the option but its receiver. The caller may add `transaction`, a hash a settler named, which the read takes as a hint.
9. **Accounts.** An account is its address's first 29 bytes; the last three name an alias of the same account. The read compares accounts so: the item's sender with the reference's, the sender with the payee, and on a charge the receiver with `payTo`.
10. **The mark** is one, `finalized`. The position is a finalized block height. The single-use value is the sender's sequence number. The read takes the account's next sequence number (`GetAccountInfo`) at the last finalized height, at the latest head (`best`) and at the position given; an account absent at a block the node holds has used no number there, and a block the node does not hold, told by `GetBlockInfo`, is `unreadable`, `history-gap`. The latest head is no mark: an item a hint names that the node answers `committed`, not yet finalized, is read at `committed`, below the mark, and answers `pending`, `below-mark` where it is the act asked about or would answer `failed`. One the node answers `finalized` under another account's signature, which no number of the sender's reaches, is read at the mark: a foreign item where it carries H. The read takes a hint only where the reader gives the height of the block that holds it and its outcome names no kind the reader does not know.
11. **Found by.** Where the next number at the last finalized height has passed the signed one, the read bisects the finalized heights from the position given to find the block at which it passed, and reads that block's items, `GetBlockItems`, each payload decoded from its binary form (`transferWithMemo`, tag 22: the receiver's 32 bytes, the memo with a 2-byte length, the amount as 8 bytes; `tokenUpdate`, tag 27: the token id with a 1-byte length and the operations with a 4-byte length, in CBOR), for the item whose header names the sender, as an account, and the signed number. Its outcome is read by its hash, `GetBlockItemStatus`. That item is the act asked about where its payload is one transfer whose memo is H, its outcome a success, its sender not the payee, and on the act `payment` its receiver `payTo`; what it moved is recorded as observed, each transfer named by the item's hash and its index among the transfers its outcome lists, whatever token or amount, zero included, with the decimals of its amount: six for CCD, whose amounts are in microCCD (concordium-grpc-api `v2/concordium/types.proto`, `Amount`), and for a PLT the `decimals` of the event's `TokenAmount` (`v2/concordium/protocol-level-tokens.proto`), which the reader gives with each PLT transfer, an outcome whose PLT transfer gives none being unparseable. For a failed result the account it names is the receiver its signed payload names, and what moved is empty. Any other item at that number consumed it: `instrument-consumed`; this payment's own `rejected` item: `transaction-failed`. The item at the number, its payload type, or an effect or event kind of its outcome, that the reader cannot decode is `unreadable`, `unparseable`.
12. **The lapse.** The number not passed at the final mark and at the latest head, while the last finalized block's own time (`slot_time`, in milliseconds) is not past the signed `expiry` (in seconds), is `pending`, `instrument-live`; not passed at the final mark and passed at the latest head, `pending`, `search-incomplete`. Not passed with a finalized block past the expiry: `instrument-unused`. Already passed at the position given: `failed`, `before-position`, naming the item at the number, which the read locates from the chain's first block; a node that does not hold the account's state or blocks below the position given answers that case `unreadable`, `history-gap`, and an item there it cannot decode `unreadable`, `unparseable`. Passed and its item not located: `pending`, `search-incomplete`.
13. **The search for H.** Every `failed` but `before-position` needs the search for H read whole and finding no act asked about. It reads every act of the sender's account after the position given: the items at the numbers from the account's next number at that position (1 where the account was absent there) to the one before its next number at the latest head; where the two are equal it is complete with no further call. Each number is located by the bisection of rule 11 and its item read and decoded as there. The first item whose payload is one transfer whose memo is H, whose outcome is a success, whose sender is not the payee and, on a charge, whose receiver is `payTo`, is the act asked about: `settled`. An item carrying H whose sender is the payee, or on a charge whose receiver is another account, is a foreign item, and so is an encrypted or scheduled transfer (tags 23 and 24) whose memo is H, another kind; a rejected one is none; an act the caller gives under the same H, by its sender and number or its hash, is never settled on. Its position is the next number to locate, `{next}`; at most 16 numbers (`CCD_SEARCH_NUMBERS`) are located in one call. A number not passed at the last finalized height, or located with no item of the account, leaves it incomplete there; an item it cannot decode, its payload type or an effect or event kind of its outcome, is named, the rest of the range is read, and the search is held at that item's number. The search reads every transaction of the sender since the position given, so a busy sender lengthens it over several calls.
14. **The payment presented.** A `settled` answer states `presented`: `yes` where the item's signed members equal those the presented payment holds, `no` where any differs. The members are compared by one digest, SHA-256 over the sender's 32 bytes, the sequence number, the expiry and the energy as 8-byte big-endian integers (the energy the header the sender signed holds, which the wire form does not: 100 for each signature, the sender's `numSignatures` and the sponsor's, plus the 94-byte sponsored header and the payload's bytes, plus `executionEnergyAmount`, as `@concordium/web-sdk` 12.0.2 computes it at signing, and as a node gives the header's `energy_amount`), a byte 1 and the sponsor's 32 bytes (a byte 0 where the header names none), and the payload in the protocol's binary form; `reference` computes it from the presented transaction, the read from the item's header and `raw_payload`. An item a hint names, read from its outcome alone below the final mark, shows neither, and is `unshown`.
15. **Not yet shown.** That every included item of an account takes its next number, none skipped; that `GetAccountInfo` answers for an account named by any of its aliases; what a node answers for an account absent at a finalized height; and the search itself, are not yet shown by a run. Until a run shows them, a search read whole proves no absence: where the read would answer `failed` for any reason but `before-position`, it answers `pending`, `search-incomplete`.
16. **The read kit.** These cases of `read-status.json` cannot arise on this read, and the read kit skips them. By their reason: `not-found`, since the read holds the sender's sequence number and answers one not passed `pending`, `instrument-live`, and one passed whose item is not located `pending`, `search-incomplete`. By the case's words: *an act read at the finality asked for, below the final mark*, since a seller names `finalized` alone, the search's latest head above it being no mark; *a read with no means to read the single-use value* and *a read that did not read the value at the latest head*, since the read holds the sequence number and reads the next number at the latest head; *a value read consumed whose transaction the search has not found*, since the search locates the item behind every number the account consumed after the position given, so one not located leaves the search with range left, held at that number, and a search read whole has found the transaction; *the validity passed with the value unconsumed, read below the final mark*, since the `expiry` is compared with a finalized block's own timestamp alone; *an act the read cannot place against the position given* (its three cases) and *an item the search could not decode that gives no position*, since every item is located at a block's height. And, while the search proves no absence (rule 15), the cases its gate holds: each case at coverage `full` whose answer on the act is `instrument-unused`, `instrument-consumed` or `transaction-failed`, which the read answers `pending`, `search-incomplete`, as the pairing's vectors hold. While the pairing's data holds no `agreement` (rule 7), the read kit runs no case on the act `agreement`: the read's agreement path is held by the vectors alone.
17. **What a record proves.** That the payer signed a Concordium transaction one of whose transfers carries H as its memo, finalized with a successful outcome. Where it transferred, one of zero included, that the payer paid the account the transfer names; it shows nothing of the amount, token or timing the ATR states. As an agreement, that this account signed the transfer carrying H.
