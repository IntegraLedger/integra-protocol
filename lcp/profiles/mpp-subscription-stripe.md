**LCP profile `mpp/subscription/stripe`: the ATR hash in the metadata of the payment that activates a Stripe subscription taken over MPP.** This profile defines how lcp places H in an MPP challenge of the `subscription` intent on the `stripe` method, and what the seller's server keeps of the subscription. No subscription request on the `stripe` method is published, so its members beyond the carrier are the seller's own, read as lcp's filter reads them; the charge on `stripe` is `mpp-charge.md`'s (rule 9).

1. Before payment the seller's MPP challenge carries H as every lcp challenge does (`mpp-charge.md`, rules 1 to 3): its `opaque` map carries `legalContext` and `legalContextUrl`, its id is H's base64url with its position, and the client checks the URL's bytes against H before it pays. The request's `methodDetails.metadata` also carries `legal_context`, whose value is `lcp:sha256:` + H. The metadata stays within Stripe's bounds: at most 50 keys, key names of at most 40 characters without square brackets, values of at most 500 characters; a challenge whose metadata could not take the key is refused `mpp/metadata-malformed`.
2. The pairing is at coverage `none`: nothing the payer or its shared payment token signs carries H, so `build` and `bound` refuse `mpp/no-signed-place`. An agreement carrying H, a payment of an x402 or MPP pairing whose payment is itself a public proof, is made first, and the seller's server creates the subscription only once that agreement has landed.
3. The seller's server creates the Stripe subscription with the payer's shared payment token and writes `legal_context` into the metadata of the payment that activates it. That activation is the subscription's one sale: one ATR covers every billing period, and every payment the seller reports on this pairing is the activation (`channel.kind` answers `open`). Later invoices are Stripe's own off-session charges under the same terms and carry no H (`channel.boundWithin` refuses `mpp/not-bound-within`); a charge on new terms is a new ATR.
4. The subscription's key is read from the activation receipt the seller's server answered the opening with, its `Payment-Receipt` payload: `channel.ref` gives `{"network":"stripe","channel":S}`, S being the receipt's `stripeSubscription`, a Stripe ID of 1 to 255 visible ASCII characters. No receipt is `mpp/receipt-missing`; a receipt whose `method` is not `stripe`, whose `status` is not `success`, or which names no such ID is `mpp/receipt-malformed`. The credential presented is not read for the key.
5. The subscription request carries no expiry, and no close is reported: `channel.until` gives none, and the subscription ends as Stripe ends it, which nothing here reads.
6. The payee broadcasts: the seller's server creates the subscription with Stripe. Nothing here reads a Stripe payment; the activation is known from the seller's report alone.
