**LCP profile `mpp/charge/nearintents`: the ATR hash in a NEAR Intents charge's `externalId`, after the agreement.** Rules 1–3, 5, 10 and 11 of `mpp/charge` apply to the challenge. The method is MPP's NEAR Intents charge (`draft-nearintents-charge-01`); the vectors are `mpp-charge-nearintents.json`.

1. **Coverage `none`.** The buyer's deposit, sent on the origin network to a deposit address the NEAR Intents backend issues for one quote, carries nothing of H, and the buyer signs nothing that carries it. H rides in the challenge request's `externalId`, as `lcp:sha256:0x` + 64 lowercase hex of H, which the resource server writes: a link, never a proof (`link: h`). The pairing is paid only after an agreement carrying H is recorded, on any agreement option the resource server offers (`agreementOn: any`), an MPP one where it offers one.
2. **No network of its own.** The deposit moves on the network `methodDetails.originNetwork` names and the backend's payout on `methodDetails.destinationNetwork`; `network` gives the origin network. The pairing is offered on whatever networks the backend serves.
3. **Absent from the first response.** The response that carries H, the ATR's link and the agreement's URL carries the agreement options and no challenge of this pairing: no deposit address exists before a quote, and none is quoted before the agreement is recorded.
4. **What the ATR names.** The ATR is assembled before any quote, so its `mpp` slot holds the challenge by the seller's fixed members alone: `currency`; `methodDetails`' `originNetwork`, `destinationNetwork`, `destinationAsset`, `destinationRecipient` and `amountOut`; and `description`, `slippageTolerance`, `settlementBackend` and `credentialTypes` where the seller sets them; with the challenge's `realm`, `method`, `intent`, `digest`, `header` and `opaque` as `mpp/charge` rule 5 reads them. `unplaced` leaves out the carrier `externalId`; the quote's `recipient`, `amount`, `methodDetails.minAmountIn`, `methodDetails.depositMemo` and `methodDetails.timeEstimate`; `methodDetails.refundTo`, which the server sets for each payer before it asks for the quote (draft, Method Details); and the challenge's `expires`, the quote's deadline. `tie` binds, and `issuedDigest` digests, the challenge as `unplaced` gives it, so every quote for one H gives one slot entry and one issued digest, and the quote's members are held by the resource server's own record of the quote (rule 9), not by lcp's check.
5. **`advertiseBeforeCarrier`, at issue.** With no quote present, it checks that the challenge carries as strings the fixed members the draft requires (`currency`, `originNetwork`, `destinationNetwork`, `destinationAsset`, `destinationRecipient`, `amountOut`), else `mpp/request-malformed`, and places H: the id and `opaque` of `mpp/charge` rules 1 and 2, and `externalId`. A challenge without `expires` is placed and given back without one. The quote's members are not read. This challenge is the one the ATR names; it is not sent.
6. **`advertise`, at each quote.** It places H in `externalId` of the quoted challenge, with its id and `opaque`, once the challenge carries as strings every member the draft requires (`amount`, `currency`, `recipient`, and `methodDetails`' four fixed members above with `minAmountIn` and `refundTo`), else `mpp/request-malformed`, and holds the quoted challenge to the challenge the ATR names, which it takes after the agreement URL: the slot's entry, the challenge rule 5 placed, or the seller's challenge before any quote. The two are compared as `unplaced` gives them, every bound member, `opaque` by its members other than the LCP ones. A quote whose fixed members differ, or one given no challenge to hold it to, is refused `near/fixed-members-differ`; an `externalId` already holding another value is refused `near/carrier-occupied`. No challenge without `recipient` is sent.
7. **The credential.** The payer broadcasts its deposit, then presents MPP's `hash` credential (`broadcaster` answers `payer`); any other `type` is refused `near/not-hash-credential`. `bound` reads H from the echoed challenge's `externalId` and holds it to the H the challenge's id and `opaque` name (`mpp/carrier-not-challenge`; an `externalId` that is not an LCP string is `near/memo-not-lcp`).
8. **`reference`.** Given the credential and the challenge it answers, it holds the credential as `bound` does, then refuses one whose echoed challenge is not that challenge, member by member, `request` and `opaque` by their decoded members, or given an option that is not a challenge, `mpp/challenge-not-option`, the one code for a credential that answers another challenge. It names the deposit by the origin network and its hash in that network's spelling, the draft's canonical transaction identifier rules (Replay Protection): on `eip155`, `0x` and 64 lowercase hex digits, read case-insensitively with or without `0x`; on `bip122`, 64 lowercase hex digits without `0x`, read the same way; on `near`, base58 of a 32-byte hash, and on `solana`, base58 of a 64-byte signature, each kept exactly; on any other namespace, for which the draft gives no rule, the hash as presented, in visible ASCII. A hash not so spelled is `mpp/credential-malformed`. The pairing has no `status`, so the reference names no payee.
9. **The resource server's order.**
    1. After the agreement is recorded, it asks the backend for a quote and issues the challenge: `recipient` the quote's single-use deposit address, `expires` at or before the quote's deadline (draft, Challenge Expiry), `externalId` H's LCP string, placed by rule 6.
    2. The ATR does not name the deposit address, and the challenge's id is set from H, so what ties an address to its challenge is the resource server's own record of the quote. It keeps one quote for each H, so no two sales share a deposit address; instances that share a quote store key it by H.
    3. It offers a challenge for H only while its own record holds no payment taken for H, and it answers itself, before the method's own server runs, a credential whose challenge has passed `expires`, whose deposit address its record holds as spent, or whose H's payment is taken, never with a fresh challenge for that H.
    4. A deposit presented in time: lcp's two checks (`mpp/charge` rule 10), then the method's verification, which moves nothing since the payer has broadcast, then its record that H's payment is taken.
    5. A quote that expires unpaid is replaced by a fresh challenge under the same ATR. A quote is unpaid only where the backend's status for its address, read after its deadline, shows no deposit; a deposit it shows is a late deposit, and no new quote is made for H while one is shown.
    6. A deposit sent in time and presented after `expires` is not handed to the method's server. The resource server takes it as a payment its payer broadcast only where the echoed `recipient` is the address of a quote its own record holds for this H and the backend's status for that address, polled to a terminal status, lists the presented hash at `SUCCESS` (draft, Settlement Procedure); until then the payment is pending. The address is never taken from the credential alone: rule 6 holds the fixed members only.
    7. A deposit the backend refunds (`FAILED`, `REFUNDED`, `INCOMPLETE_DEPOSIT`) is never taken, presented in time or late. The buyer pays again on a fresh challenge under the same ATR (draft, Client Recovery): a fresh quote changes only members the ATR does not name, so it binds no new terms.
10. **No read.** No public payer-signed act carries H, and the deposit pays the quote's address and not the payee, so lcp declares no `read`, `status`, `recover` or `fetchPresented` for the pairing. It declares inapplicable every case of the read kit that reads (`read-status.md`): no case of the kit runs through it, and it has no form. The resource server's own record settles it.
11. What a record of this pairing proves: before this payment was reported, an agreement transaction carrying this ATR's hash was signed on a network, recorded in a transaction; this shows neither that its signer is the payer nor when the deposit was made. The seller's MPP challenge carried the hash in its `externalId`. The deposit carries no hash, and its payer signed nothing that does. The seller reported it paid. `nonce` is `unshown`, and `confirmed` is `no` until a recorded run shows the form.
