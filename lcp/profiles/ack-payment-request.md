**LCP profile `ack/payment-request`.** This profile targets ACK protocol version 2025-05-04. 1. The server's Payment Request `id` is `lcp:sha256:H`, signed in its Payment Request token, and the 402 body carries `"legalContext":{"type":"sha256","value":H,"legalContextUrl":L}` beside it. The body names the token `paymentRequestToken`, as ACK's SDKs write it from 0.10.0, or `paymentToken`, as they wrote it before (`agentcommercekit/ack` commit `27e3ffb`, 2025-09-01; at its parent `6fc3247`, `packages/ack-pay/src/create-payment-request-body.ts:28-31`). A reader takes either name; a body that carries both carries one token under both names, and one whose two members differ is refused as a malformed token is. 2. The client reads H from the signed token and compares it with `legalContext.value` as 32 decoded bytes, then fetches L, hashes what it receives and compares again; on any mismatch it does not pay. 3. The client signs nothing that carries H: ACK defines no payer signature. 4. After settlement the receipt's `credentialSubject` carries the token, under either name by the same rule (`create-payment-receipt.ts:30-33` at `6fc3247`; `:30` at `3db94d6`), with H as its `id`. 5. A stock ACK client reads the 402 body as an object of `paymentRequestToken` and `paymentRequest` alone and drops `legalContext` without refusing it (`demos/payments/src/index.ts:165-171` at `3db94d6`), so a buyer whose client is stock never sees L. A client that is to check the ATR before it pays, or to make an agreement first, reads `legalContext` itself.
