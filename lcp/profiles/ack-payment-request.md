**LCP profile `ack/payment-request`.** 1. The server's Payment Request `id` is `lcp:sha256:H`, signed in its `paymentRequestToken`, and the 402 body carries `"legalContext":{"type":"sha256","value":H,"legalContextUrl":L}` beside it. 2. The client reads H from the signed token and compares it with `legalContext.value` as 32 decoded bytes, then fetches L, hashes what it receives and compares again; on any mismatch it does not pay. 3. The client signs nothing that carries H: ACK defines no payer signature. 4. After settlement the receipt's `credentialSubject.paymentRequestToken` carries H as the token's `id`.
