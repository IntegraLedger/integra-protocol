**LCP profile `x402/batch-settlement/solana`: the ATR hash as the seller Memo instruction of a payment channel's opening.** x402's `batch-settlement` scheme on Solana (`scheme_batch_settlement_svm.md` at `x402-foundation/x402` `751590a2`).

1. The option's `extra.memo` is `lcp:sha256:0x` + 64 lowercase hex of H, 77 UTF-8 bytes, which the stock client writes as the seller Memo instruction of the channel's opening, beside the scheme's own receiver-authorizer memo (`@x402/svm` 2.30.0, `src/batch-settlement/client/builders.ts:94`). `bound` holds the presented `accepted` to the option filter and refuses a transaction unless exactly one of its top-level Memo instructions holds an LCP string and it is H's, the receiver-authorizer memo beside it not read, or whose payment-channels instruction is no `open` (`x402/not-an-opening`): no LCP-form memo is `svm/no-carrier`, more than one `svm/memo-count`, one spelled otherwise `svm/carrier-not-canonical`, one other than `extra.memo` `svm/carrier-mismatch`; a durable nonce account loaded from an address table is `svm/nonce-account-not-static`. `reference` names the message's digest, the buyer, the fee payer, H, the option's payee and the blockhash or durable nonce, and refuses a payment whose `accepted` is not the option it is given, `x402/accepted-not-option`.
2. Deposits after the opening, vouchers and the close carry no H, and the instrument is `unshown`: no vector or run shows yet that the signed opening lands once.
3. The client builds the highest message version both `extra.transactionVersions` and lcp admit: version 1 where the list holds 1, version 0 where it holds 0 alone or is absent, as a stock client does (`@x402/svm` at `88b66dd4`, `utils.ts:200-203`); a list that admits neither is refused at the filter (`x402/option-malformed`). `bound` and `reference` decode legacy, version 0 and version 1 messages, and refuse one whose version the list does not admit, `svm/version-not-admitted`, `legacy` being admitted where the list holds 0 and the list absent being `[0]`, as `@x402/svm` 2.31.0's facilitator admits it (`isAcceptedTransactionVersion`): a payment the option does not admit is no answer to it.
4. An option carrying `extra.splits` is refused at the filter, `svm/input-malformed`, before the buyer signs: no Solana build writes split legs, and each pays the whole amount to `payTo`.
5. The read, `status`, as the profile `read-status` answers it: one mark, `finalized`, the default and the final mark. The search for H lists the memo-carrying signatures of the buyer's own account, which every transaction the buyer signs loads, never the fee payer's, from the issue slot to the latest head, `confirmed`, which is no mark: 10 pages of 1,000 and 50 candidates a pass, a pass that meets either bound, or a candidate no lookup finds or one that does not decode, holding a handle the next read resumes from, below the pages it read, with the transactions met that the answer rests on, a consumer of the value or one the payer signed, other than those the caller gave, which the next read looks up by signature. An act met only above the `finalized` slot is `pending`, `below-mark`, named with no finality. The parties come from the landed transaction: who signed it, and the payee and the distribution the `open` names, its payee being its third account, a seed of the channel's address, which the program's `distribute` pays: its deposit is recorded as the program's `distribute` pays it out, one share for each account it pays more than nothing: each recipient its bps of the deposit and the `open`'s payee the remainder of 10,000 bps, each rounded down, an account named twice taking both, each share with that account as its beneficiary and the channel's account as its contract, the rounding dust the program sweeps to its treasury recorded for no account; it counts where the program pays the option's payee more than nothing, an `open` with no distribution, which leaves its payee the whole, among them, and names `other` where it pays the option's payee nothing, whatever account the `open` names as its payee; each transfer of what moved is named by the transaction and its instruction's place, the top-level index or `<index>.<place>` for an inner instruction, and a transaction with a failed result names the destination the payer signed and moved nothing. `presented` compares the landed message with the one presented, by its digest, or with the transaction presented alone. The single-use value is the signed message: consumed by its own landing or by its durable nonce advanced; unused once its blockhash has lapsed, `isBlockhashValid` answering `false` at `finalized` at a `finalized` slot at least 150 past the context slot of the first `isBlockhashValid` answer of `true` at `confirmed`, which the search's handle keeps, a slot count alone deciding nothing, since a skipped slot adds no block and the chain ends the blockhash's validity 150 blocks after its own, and a blockhash read invalid before such an answer deciding nothing; a durable-nonce message never lapses. Until a run shows every transaction the buyer signs listed under its account, a search read whole proves no absence: no `failed` but `before-position` is answered, `pending`, `search-incomplete` in its place.
6. Declared inapplicable to the read kit, cases the read cannot meet on Solana: the case settled at a finality asked for below the final mark, since a seller names `finalized` alone; while the search proves no absence, every case at coverage `full` whose answer is `instrument-unused`, `instrument-consumed` or `transaction-failed`, which the read answers `pending`, `search-incomplete` (`vectors/svm-read.json`); each transfer of what moved is named by its transaction and its place in it, since the act is an opening that holds exactly one `open`, beside which only `top_up`s may stand, whose one deposit into the channel's token account is what moved, recorded as its shares at the `open`'s place, a transaction holding more than one `open` being no act of this pairing; an act of the pairing's kind that names no account, since every `open` names its payee; a payment act carrying H that transferred zero, since the program's `open` refuses a deposit of zero, and a share that rounds down to zero, which the program pays nothing, is read as no payment to the payee, by design setting aside for it `read-status` rule 9's zero; and, the read holding the signed message, a read that did not read the value at the latest head, since its search and its lookups read on to `confirmed`, and a value whose validity passed, unconsumed at the final mark and consumed at the latest head, since a blockhash message is consumed only by its own transaction, which that read finds, and a durable-nonce message has no lapse.
7. The payment-channels program is on devnet only: on mainnet the pairing waits until a read shows the program deployed there. It is never an agreement option.
8. What a record of this pairing proves: the payer's own signed act carries this ATR's hash: a Solana transaction the buyer's account signed, whose seller Memo instruction (`extra.memo`), beside the scheme's receiver-authorizer memo, is the hash's LCP string, final at `finalized` with no error, which deposited the amount its `open` names into a payment channel whose distribution pays the option's payee more than nothing, recorded with its mint and each account's share of the amount as the program pays it out. The memo is in the transaction's instruction data on chain. It compares no amount, mint or time with the ATR's terms.
