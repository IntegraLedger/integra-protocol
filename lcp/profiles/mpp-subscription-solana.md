**LCP profile `mpp/subscription/solana`: the ATR hash as the Memo instruction of a Solana subscription's activation.** MPP's `subscription` intent under the `solana` method, as `@solana/mpp` 0.7.0 defines it (`src/Methods.ts:129-201`). Rules 1–3 and 5 of `mpp/charge` apply to the challenge.

1. The request's `externalId` is `lcp:sha256:0x` + 64 lowercase hex of H, 77 UTF-8 bytes. A request whose `externalId` is present with another value is refused `svm/carrier-occupied`.
2. The stock client writes `externalId` as a Memo Program instruction (`MemoSq4gqABAXKb96qnH8TysNcWxMyWCqXgDLGmfcHr`) of the activation, the transaction it signs, after `transfer_subscription` (`src/client/Subscription.ts:240-242`); a memo past 566 bytes is refused there, and here at the filter.
3. The stock server never reads the Memo, so the resource server makes this check before the stock verify, as `bound`: among the activation's top-level Memo instructions, exactly one has data that parses as an LCP string, spelled exactly as rule 1 gives (`svm/carrier-not-canonical`), equal to the echoed request's `externalId` (`svm/carrier-mismatch`) and naming the H of the challenge's id (`mpp/carrier-not-challenge`). None is `svm/no-carrier`, more than one `svm/memo-count`.
4. The challenge: `amount` a positive u64; `currency` and `recipient` base58 keys; `periodUnit` `day` or `week` and `periodCount` a decimal integer, at most 365 days or 52 weeks, as the stock client maps the period to the program's 1 to 8,760 hours (`src/shared/subscription.ts:14-40`); `subscriptionExpires`, where present, an RFC 3339 date-time; in `methodDetails`: `decimals` an integer from 0 to 255; `mint`, `planId` and `puller` keys; `tokenProgram` Token or Token-2022; `programId` and `recentBlockhash` keys where present; `network` `mainnet`, `devnet` or `localnet` (absent: mainnet), `localnet` naming no CAIP-2 network, so that a read or a reference on it is `svm/network-undeclared`; and `feePayer` a boolean, `true` only with a `feePayerKey`. Any other challenge is refused `mpp/request-malformed`.
5. The credential is `type="transaction"`, the base64 of the activation the subscriber signed. Its `transfer_subscription` takes the challenge's `puller`, the seller's key, as a signer (`src/client/Subscription.ts:230`, its signer role at `:340`), so the client cannot broadcast it alone: the seller's server co-signs and submits it, and the pairing's `broadcaster` answers `payee`. A credential of another type, `signature` included, is refused `mpp/credential-type`.
6. The sale is the activation: the transaction that subscribes the payer to the plan and collects the first period. Renewals are the seller's later pulls; none carries H, none is read, and a credential within the subscription is refused `mpp/not-bound-within`. The subscription a payment opens is the account the activation's `subscribe` instruction names as the subscription, its fourth account (`src/client/Subscription.ts:313-320`, the subscription at `:317`). The buyer `reference` names, whose account the read's search pages and whose signature is the act's, is the subscriber that instruction names first, a writable signer (`:314`), whose token account `transfer_subscription` debits; never the `puller`, which signs beside it, a signer taken by its place being no party: past the fee payer `@solana/kit` 8.4.0 orders the signers by address (`transaction-messages/src/compile/v0/accounts.ts:225-236`). An activation with no one `subscribe` instruction of the request's program is `svm/open-not-found`.
7. The instrument is `reusable`: a bearer proof of the subscription is presented again. The pairing is never an agreement option.
8. What a record of this pairing proves: the payer's own signed act carries this ATR's hash: a Solana transaction the buyer's account signed, a subscription's activation, whose one LCP-form Memo instruction is the hash's LCP string, final at `finalized` with no error, which paid the first period to an account the option's payee owns, recorded with its mint and amount. The memo is in the transaction's instruction data on chain. Renewals are the seller's later pulls, which do not carry the hash. It compares no amount, mint, period or time with the ATR's terms.
9. The read, `status`, as the profile `read-status` answers it: one mark, `finalized`, the default and the final mark. The search for H lists the memo-carrying signatures of the buyer's own account, which every transaction the buyer signs loads, never the fee payer's, from the issue slot to the latest head, `confirmed`, which is no mark: 10 pages of 1,000 and 50 candidates a pass, a pass that meets either bound, or a candidate no lookup finds or one that does not decode, holding a handle the next read resumes from, below the pages it read, with the transactions met that the answer rests on, a consumer of the value or one the payer signed, other than those the caller gave, which the next read looks up by signature. An act met only above the `finalized` slot is `pending`, `below-mark`, named with no finality. The parties come from the landed transaction: who signed it, and the owner, as the transaction's token balances name it (`meta.postTokenBalances`), of the token account a transfer from the buyer's token account reaches, top-level or inner; a payment counts where one such transfer reaches the option's payee, zero included, and an agreement is held to no account; each transfer of what moved is named by the transaction and its instruction's place, the top-level index or `<index>.<place>` for an inner instruction, and a transaction with a failed result names the destination the payer signed and moved nothing. `presented` compares the landed message with the one presented, by its digest, or with the transaction presented alone. The single-use value is the signed message: consumed by its own landing or by its durable nonce advanced; unused once its blockhash has lapsed, `isBlockhashValid` answering `false` at `finalized` at a `finalized` slot at least 150 past the context slot of the first `isBlockhashValid` answer of `true` at `confirmed`, which the search's handle keeps, a slot count alone deciding nothing, since a skipped slot adds no block and the chain ends the blockhash's validity 150 blocks after its own, and a blockhash read invalid before such an answer deciding nothing; a durable-nonce message never lapses. Until a run shows every transaction the buyer signs listed under its account, a search read whole proves no absence: no `failed` but `before-position` is answered, `pending`, `search-incomplete` in its place.
10. Declared inapplicable to the read kit, cases the read cannot meet on Solana: the case settled at a finality asked for below the final mark, since a seller names `finalized` alone; while the search proves no absence, every case at coverage `full` whose answer is `instrument-unused`, `instrument-consumed` or `transaction-failed`, which the read answers `pending`, `search-incomplete` (`vectors/svm-read.json`); and, the read holding the signed message, a read that did not read the value at the latest head, since its search and its lookups read on to `confirmed`, and a value whose validity passed, unconsumed at the final mark and consumed at the latest head, since a blockhash message is consumed only by its own transaction, which that read finds, and a durable-nonce message has no lapse.
