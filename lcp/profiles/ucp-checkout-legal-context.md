**LCP profile `ucp/checkout/legal-context`: the ATR hash as a UCP link.**

1. The checkout response carries one `links[]` entry `{"type":"legal_context","url":L,"title":"lcp:sha256:"+H}`. H is the SHA-256 of the ATR's exact bytes, as `0x` lowercase hex, and L is an `https` URL that serves those bytes.
2. The entry is written before `ap2.merchant_authorization` is computed, so the business's signature covers it.
3. The platform fetches L, hashes the bytes received, and compares the result with H as 32 decoded bytes. On a mismatch it does not present the checkout for approval.
4. Under the AP2 Mandates extension the checkout mandate commits to the checkout, and so to H. Without it, the buyer's approval is not signed, and a record of the payment says so.
5. The checkout `id` is the business's own and is never H. Each checkout state that changes the terms carries the entry of its own ATR, whose binding slot names the checkout: its `ucp` slot, by the checkout `id`; or, where the checkout is paid through a payment handler whose pairing commits to the request, the committed request's path.
6. Under the AP2 Mandates extension the checkout JWT whose hash, `checkout_hash`, the buyer's checkout mandate carries, and which is presented beside the mandate, is the business's detached `ap2.merchant_authorization` with its payload reinserted: `header.payload.signature`, where `header` and `signature` are the two segments of `ap2.merchant_authorization` and `payload` is the base64url, without padding, of the RFC 8785 form of the checkout without its `ap2` member, the bytes that signature covers (UCP 2026-08-25, `docs/specification/payment/extensions/ap2-mandates.md`, *Business Authorization*, `sign_checkout`, at tag `v2026-08-25`, commit `87dd4b1`). UCP does not say which bytes are the checkout JWT; this is the second of the two readings its open issue 905 sets out, and this item is provisional until that issue is settled. The checkout's `legal_context` link is read from that payload.
7. A business does not validate `ap2.checkout_mandate` against the pattern UCP's schema gives it at `v2026-08-25` (`source/schemas/common/payment_ap2_mandate.json:18`): that pattern refuses the presentation AP2's SDK writes, whose hops are joined by `~~` and whose closed mandate ends in `~` (AP2 `v0.2.0`, `code/sdk/python/ap2/sdk/mandate.py:396-400`). The mandate is read as AP2 writes it: one SD-JWT, or the open mandate and the closed mandate joined by `~~`.
8. Without the AP2 Mandates extension (`ucp/checkout/unsigned`) nothing the payer presents carries H. The business names the charge by its own reference for it, as `card-seller-reference.md` rule 5 states: `reference` takes `{"charge":R}`, R of 1 to 255 visible ASCII characters, with the checkout option the charge answers, `{"checkout":C}`, and gives `{"checkout":C,"charge":R}`. A presentation that is not an object whose `charge` has that form is `ucp/charge-reference-malformed`; then an option that is not `{"checkout":C}`, C of 1 to 256 characters, is `ucp/option-malformed`. Nothing the charge moved is compared with the option.
