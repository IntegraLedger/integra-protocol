**LCP profile `x402/exact/starknet`: the ATR hash's low 250 bits as the SNIP-9 nonce.**

**The form**

1. The `PaymentRequired` carries `extensions.legalContext.info` = `{"type":"sha256","value":H,"legalContextUrl":L}`, H the SHA-256 of the ATR's exact bytes as `0x` lowercase hex and L an `https` URL serving them.
2. The client fetches L, hashes the bytes received, and compares with H as 32 bytes. On a mismatch it does not sign.
3. On a match it signs SNIP-12 revision 1 typed data for x402's `OutsideExecution` (SNIP-9 version 2), under the domain `name` `Account.execute_from_outside`, `version` 2, `revision` 1 and `chainId` the network's reference as a short string, the option's `network` being `starknet:SN_MAIN` or `starknet:SN_SEPOLIA`: `Caller` the option's `extra.feePayer`; `Nonce` = H AND (2^250 − 1), the low 250 bits of H, as a felt; `Execute After` 1; `Execute Before` the client's clock plus the option's `maxTimeoutSeconds`, in Unix seconds; and `Calls` exactly one call, `To` the option's `asset`, `Selector` sn_keccak(`transfer`), `Calldata` `[payTo, the amount's low 128 bits, its high 128 bits]`. It presents the payer's account as `from`, the typed data and the signature as 1 to 32 felts, and echoes `extensions` unchanged. A felt is `0x` and 1 to 64 hex digits below the STARK prime 2^251 + 17·2^192 + 1, compared as a number. That is how the client writes a felt. In a presented payment's `message`, `Execute After`, `Execute Before` and each call's `To` and `Calldata` are also read as a decimal string or a non-negative safe integer, as starknet.js writes them (its `CallData.compile` writes calldata in decimal) and as SNIP-12 hashes them, to the same value; a `Selector` is read only as hex, since SNIP-12 hashes any other string in a `selector` as a function name. The 250 bits are lcp's choice within the 251 a felt holds whole.
4. **The agreement's own nonce.** Where the payment is offered as an agreement, the signed `Nonce` is the low 250 bits of the agreement's value in place of H's: SHA-256 over the 16 ASCII bytes `lcp-agreement-v1` followed by H's 32 bytes (the profile `read-status`, rule 1), AND (2^250 − 1); its vectors are `core-vectors.json`'s `agreementValue` and this pairing's. The payer's account keys a used nonce by its value alone, so an agreement and a payment under one H from one account never take one nonce. `build` writes it for the act `agreement`; `bound`, given the act and H, refuses a payment whose `Nonce` is not the value that act carries, `starknet/nonce-not-bound`, and gives H where it is; `reference`, given the same, names that value as the signed nonce. With nothing given, `bound` takes H from the echoed legal context and gives it where its low 250 bits are the `Nonce`, as a payment carries it.
5. The server refuses a payment whose `Nonce` is not the low 250 bits of an H it issued for this request, or of that H's agreement value where it offered the option as an agreement, and has not seen claimed. It refuses a payment whose `from` is the option's `payTo`: an outside execution the payee's account signed is no payment and no agreement. `bound` refuses a signed message whose `Caller` is not the option's `extra.feePayer`, `starknet/caller-not-fee-payer`, and one with a call whose `Selector` is not sn_keccak(`transfer`), `starknet/selector-not-transfer`: who may execute it and the form that carries H, never what it pays.
6. A settler executes the outside execution as the named `Caller` and pays the fee, through that account or the relayer behind it where the `Caller` is a forwarder; nothing else in the signed message names it. It checks the payment by x402's Starknet scheme before anything is sent (rule 25), and the server checks H first (rule 5).

**What "succeeded" means, and what fails**

7. Succeeded: the receipt's execution status is `SUCCEEDED`, and the transaction's trace (`starknet_traceTransaction`) holds, at any depth, below a forwarder as below the executor, an `execute_from_outside_v2` invocation on the payer's account whose calldata carries the signed nonce, no invocation on the path to it reverted. It consumed the nonce. Each `Transfer` the receipt holds from the payer, on any token, is what moved, with its token, recipient and amount; and a `transfer` call of zero under that invocation for which its token emitted no `Transfer` is a transfer of zero to the account its calldata names. None is counted, and no token or amount is compared.
8. `REVERTED`: the transaction is included, and the signature check and the nonce's use inside `execute_from_outside_v2` roll back with it (x402's scheme, `scheme_exact_starknet.md`). No payment and no agreement, and the nonce is not consumed: the outside execution can still land until `Execute Before`.
9. `SUCCEEDED` with the `execute_from_outside_v2` invocation reverted beneath a forwarder: the same outcome. A reverted invocation's subtree is rolled back with it, its calls keeping `is_reverted: false`, and the nonce is not consumed.
10. A `Transfer` is an event whose first key is sn_keccak(`Transfer`), keyed (keys `[selector, from, to]`, data `[low, high]`) or unkeyed (keys `[selector]`, data `[from, to, low, high]`), each amount limb below 2^128; its token is the contract that emitted it. Any other event with that first key is of neither layout.

**The read** (the profile `read-status`)

11. The reference names the network, the payer's account `from`, the asset, which is the event search's key, the signed nonce (H's low 250 bits, or the agreement value's), the option's `payTo` as the payee, `Execute Before` as `settleBy`, and the outside execution as presented: `caller`, `nonce`, `executeAfter`, `executeBefore` and `calls`, each call's `to`, `selector` and `calldata`, every member a felt in its shortest lowercase spelling. `reference` refuses a presented `accepted` that is not the option it is given, `x402/accepted-not-option`. The caller adds H for `status`, by which the read tells H's other value (the agreement value's low bits on a payment, H's on an agreement) from the act's own, and may add the transaction a settler named. The reference's nonce must be the low 250 bits of the value the act asked about carries, H's on a payment and the agreement value's on an agreement, and it must hold the outside execution as presented, each member a felt; otherwise `status` answers `unreadable`, `unparseable`, with nothing read.
12. The marks are `ACCEPTED_ON_L2`, the newest block (`latest`), and `ACCEPTED_ON_L1`, the final mark. A `PRE_CONFIRMED` receipt, or one that names no block, is below every mark: no act is found in it, and the read of the nonce decides. A position is a block number.
13. Where the reference names a transaction, it is read first, its receipt and its trace, and classified as rules 16 to 18 classify a transaction the search finds. One that did not succeed, one that carries no outside execution under either of H's values, and one the node does not know decide nothing, and the read goes on. A receipt or trace of it the read cannot decode is `unreadable`, `unparseable`; one beyond lcp's bounds, `unreadable`, `too-large`; a receipt with no trace, `unreadable`, `history-gap`. Each other act the caller gives by its transaction (the profile `read-status`, rule 6), at most 16 a call, is read the same way and holds no search: one the read can decode is classified as rule 18 classifies it; one whose receipt or trace it cannot decode, that exceeds lcp's bounds, or that has no trace is named among the foreign items, `given`, by its transaction alone (rule 19 of `read-status`); one the node does not know decides nothing. A query that gives more than 16 is `unreadable`, `too-large`, with nothing read.
14. The read is of the nonce, as state: `is_valid_outside_execution_nonce(nonce)` on the payer's account at the newest `ACCEPTED_ON_L1` block and at the newest block, each read with its number and its own timestamp. Valid at both: no outside execution under the nonce has landed on the payer's account, whatever token it moved; the account keeps one nonce space for every token and the nonce derives from H alone, so these two reads are the search for H, read whole (the profile `read-status`, rule 26). Where the final block's own timestamp is before `Execute Before`, the answer is `pending`, `instrument-live`, save where the newest block's own timestamp is at or past it: the validity has passed as read below the final mark, and the answer is `pending`, `below-mark` (the profile `read-status`, rule 17), until a final block shows it. Where the final block's own timestamp is at or past `Execute Before`, the answer is `failed`, `instrument-unused`. Where the caller gave a search position, a `pending` answer returns one that starts after the newest block read. A block whose state the node does not serve is `unreadable`, `history-gap`. Where the node answers the call with `starknet_call`'s documented `CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND` (20), an account not deployed at that block, or `ENTRYPOINT_NOT_FOUND` (21), an account with no SNIP-9 entry point, which the pairing requires of the payer, the account runs no outside execution at that block, so the nonce reads valid there: before `Execute Before` the answer is `pending`, `instrument-live`, since the account can still be deployed or given a SNIP-9 class and run it, and after it, read so at the final block and at the newest, `failed`, `instrument-unused`. The cost, stated: an account that ran the payment and then replaced its class with one that has no such entry point reads as one that never ran it. Any other error is `unreadable`, `transport`: an error is never absence, and the read establishes nothing.
15. Consumed at either block: the search finds the transaction that consumed it. It reads `starknet_getEvents` on the asset for events whose first key is sn_keccak(`Transfer`), a page at a time by the node's continuation (at most 8 pages and 16 candidate transactions a call), from the position the caller gave, or block 0 where it gave none, to the block at which the nonce was read consumed: the final block where it was consumed there, else the newest. Each `Transfer` from the payer names a candidate transaction, whose receipt and trace it reads; a candidate is taken only where its trace carries an outside execution under either of H's values. Where range is left, the answer carries the search's position, from which the next call resumes. A range the node does not hold is `unreadable`, `history-gap`. A candidate whose receipt or trace the read cannot decode, or that exceeds lcp's bounds, and an event of the asset with the `Transfer` key of neither layout, is an item the search could not decode: it holds the search at its page, and the answer names it (the profile `read-status`, rule 19). The account keys the nonce alone, so a copy the payer signs with a later `Execute Before` consumes it too: `Execute Before` bounds the lapse and not the search.
16. The payer's outside execution under the act's own nonce consumed it, and it is the one transaction that did, so once it is found the search is whole: no candidate after it is read, and no bound of rule 15 cuts the search short. On the act `agreement` it is the act whatever it moved, an agreement being held to no account. On the act `payment` it is the act where one of its `Transfer`s from the payer, or a transfer of zero (rule 7), reaches `payTo`, whatever its token or amount, zero included. A payment execution that names no payee's account, its transfers reaching other accounts or it holding none, consumed the nonce and is not the act: at the final mark it is `failed`, `instrument-consumed`, named with what it moved and the reason `other-account`; below it, `pending`, `below-mark`. Where its receipt holds an event with the `Transfer` key of neither layout, it is not shown to name no payee's account, and it is an item the read could not decode (rule 15), or on a named transaction `unreadable`, `unparseable`.
17. Consumed, and the search read to its end with no such transaction found: `pending`, `search-incomplete`, never `not-found` or `failed`. The search reads the asset's events alone, so an execution under the nonce that moved another token, or emitted no `Transfer`, is found only where the reference names its transaction: no index leads from a nonce to the execution that used it.
18. Every other outside execution under either of H's values that succeeded in a transaction the read met is a foreign item, named with the first reason of the closed list that holds: the payee's account signed it, `payee-signed`; another account, `other-signer`; one the caller gave, by its reference or its transaction, `given`; the payer's under H's other value, `other-kind`. The search is keyed on the payer's `Transfer`s, so another account's execution is met only in a named transaction. A transaction that did not succeed decides nothing and is no foreign item. A transaction at or before the position given that is the act or consumed the nonce answers `failed`, `before-position`, naming it.
19. What moved names each `Transfer` by the transaction and the event's index among its receipt's `events` (`event`), and a transfer of zero read from the trace by the transaction and the indexes of the calls that lead to it from the trace's root (`call`); each with its token, recipient and amount. The recipient alone is compared, with `payTo`, on the act `payment`. The signer is the account whose `execute_from_outside_v2` executed.
20. `presented`, on `settled`: `yes` where the executed outside execution's `Caller`, `Nonce`, `Execute After`, `Execute Before` and `Calls`, read from the `execute_from_outside_v2` calldata, signature apart, equal the outside execution as presented; `no` where one differs; `unshown` where the calldata does not show them whole. It decides no answer.
21. A lost settle answer is decided by the read of the nonce and the search, with no transaction hash, save where rule 17 holds.
22. `transaction-failed` and `not-found` are never answered. The read kit's cases this ledger cannot give are declared inapplicable to it: `transaction-failed` and the cases opening *a failed result that consumed the value*, since neither failed outcome consumes the nonce (rules 8 and 9); and `not-found`, since the read always holds the nonce (rule 14). Declared inapplicable also, by the cases' words: *a read with no means to read the single-use value* and *a read that did not read the value at the latest head*, since the read holds the nonce and reads it at a final block and at the latest block (rule 14); *an act the read cannot place against the position given* (its three cases) and *an item the search could not decode that gives no position*, since every event the search reads names its block (rule 15); *an act found above the final head*, *a search narrowed to one asset, payee or token*, *the validity passed with the value unconsumed and the search for H not read whole*, *instrument-live is answered before search-incomplete*, *an item the search could not decode holds a failed answer*, *instrument-live is answered before an item the search could not decode* and *a second act of the payer that meets every test*, since the nonce is H's low bits, or the agreement value's, keyed by the account alone, so its read at the final mark and at the latest head is the search for H, read whole, which no item changes, a nonce read unconsumed is read with no other search and so meets no item, and every act of the payer that carries H runs under that nonce, which one act consumes, the `Transfer` search that looks for the transaction that consumed a nonce read consumed being no search for H, which can be left with range to read and then answers `pending`, `search-incomplete` at its position (rules 14 to 17); and, on the act `payment`, *an act carrying H that names no account and consumed nothing* and *at coverage full, instrument-consumed waits for the search for H*, since every act of the payer that carries H runs under that nonce and so consumes it, and that read is the search (rules 14 and 15). The case of a validity passed read below the final mark arises (rule 14), and so does a given transaction the read cannot decode, named `given` (rule 13), and so do *transactions carrying H with a failed result*, the payer's own and one the payee signed, since a `REVERTED` transaction is included in its block (rule 8) and any account can sign an outside execution that carries H: with the nonce valid, the read meets them where they are named or given by their transaction, and neither decides anything (rules 13 and 18). *search-incomplete is answered before not-found* arises and is not declared: the read holds the nonce, so the case, whose facts give no value, is the nonce read consumed with no transaction found and the `Transfer` search left with range to read (rule 15), which the profile `read-status`'s order of the `pending` reasons answers alike, `search-incomplete` at the search's position.

**Recovery**

23. `recover` takes `{network, tx, option}`, the option's `asset` naming the token, and returns the nonce of the transaction's executed outside execution whose calls reach a `transfer` on that token, as `0x` and 64 hex digits: H's low 250 bits from a payment's transaction, and from an agreement's the agreement value's, which a holder of the ATR computes from its own H and compares. The ledger holds 250 of H's bits, so nothing recovers H itself. Its refusals: a reader for another network, or a `tx` or `asset` that is not a felt, `starknet/wrong-reader`; a failed or malformed read, or a trace beyond lcp's bounds, which it reads no further, `starknet/unreadable`; no receipt, `starknet/not-found`; at `ACCEPTED_ON_L2` or `ACCEPTED_ON_L1`, a reverted transaction `starknet/reverted`, no such execution `starknet/no-transfer`, and executions under two nonces `starknet/ambiguous`, each of which a `PRE_CONFIRMED` receipt, or one that names no block, gives as `starknet/not-final`.

**The agreement**

24. The payment can be an agreement: one outside execution the payer signs, paying the payee directly a fixed amount, with no hold, landing whole on one network. The floor is 0 on a token a run has shown to emit a `Transfer` for a transfer of zero on the option's network, since the search finds a payment by its `Transfer` alone, and 1 base unit on any other: on Starknet Sepolia, STRK (`0x4718f5a0fc34cc1af16a1cdee98ffb20c31f5cd61d6ab07201858f4287c938d`) and USDC (`0x53b40a647cedfca6ca84f542a0fe36736031905a9639a7f19a3c1e66bfd5080`), each of whose `transfer(to, 0)` emits a `Transfer` of 0 (`starknet_simulateTransactions`, `test/fixtures/starknet-zero-transfer-sepolia.json`); no run has shown one on Starknet mainnet. The option filter takes an amount of `"0"` on a token whose floor is 0 and refuses it on any other. `starknetFloor` evaluates the rule for a token and a network, answering 1 where no network is given, and refuses an asset that is not a felt (`starknet/felt-malformed`) and a network that is not Starknet's (`starknet/network-malformed`). The settler pays the fee, always, through the `Caller` fixed in what the payer signs. The transaction must succeed (rule 7). An outside execution the payee's account signed is no agreement.

**What a settler checks**

25. x402's Starknet scheme (`scheme_exact_starknet.md`) states a settler's checks: the typed data and its `Caller`, the token and the recipient against the option, the signature's validity and the nonce's state through the payer's account, a simulation, and, after the transaction, the one `Transfer` of the option's asset to `payTo` for the amount. The server's check of H (rule 5) comes before every one of them, and before anything moves.

**What a record proves**

26. A settled read shows that the payer's own signed SNIP-9 outside execution carries H's low 250 bits as its nonce (on an agreement, the agreement value's), which the payer's account verified with its signature when it executed it; and that it paid the account each `Transfer`, or a transfer of zero, names the amount it states, zero included: on a payment, `payTo` among them; where the read observed no transfer, that the payer signed it. A holder of the ATR confirms H's low 250 bits from the nonce; the ledger holds no more of H. It does not show that the amount, the asset or the timing match the ATR's content.
